# Production strategy before execution

Status: adopted production-wide operating principle at Director exchange184, 2026-10-05. Applies to current agent work and future Factory orchestration; automated enforcement is not yet implemented.

Before substantial production work, establish a credible path from the intended audience experience to a finished, revisable and appropriately reusable deliverable. Test the assumptions most likely to invalidate that path before detailed execution. Local corrections must support the strategy, not gradually replace it with an unexamined chain of compensating fixes.

## Scope and responsibility

Apply this to story and character development, visual design, assets, performance, simulation, camera, lighting, voice, music, sound, editing, distribution and Factory software. Work backwards from the trailer and episodes1/2, or the applicable accepted production objective. A component is useful because it enables a production outcome, not merely because it can be built.

The agent owns technical strategy, sequencing, diagnosis and internal review. The Director owns material creative choices, quality acceptance, scope and release priorities, and spending or other authority boundaries. A strategy brief is not a new routine approval gate. Proceed with authorized reversible work; do not ask the Director to choose algorithms. Challenge an incompatible plan before costly commitment, including the agent's own approach.

## Required strategy brief

Keep one concise current brief in the existing work package, linking established records instead of copying them. Before substantial execution, answer:

1. **Outcome and acceptance:** What screen, listening, story, audience or operational result must this achieve? What observable evidence would pass or fail it? What remains protected, and what may vary?
2. **Complete path and dependencies:** How will the result be authored, integrated, reviewed, revised and delivered? Identify upstream inputs, downstream consumers and critical interfaces. Include the next required use, not just the easiest isolated demonstration.
3. **Chosen approach and rationale:** Why should this representation/workflow/tool combination achieve the result? Consider materially different alternatives where uncertainty warrants them; do not invent alternatives for routine work. Distinguish measured capability, creative judgment and untested assumptions.
4. **Highest risks and earliest proof:** Which assumptions could invalidate the approach? Design the smallest representative integrated test that exposes them, with evidence needed to continue, change strategy or stop. Diagnostic unit tests may support it, but cannot replace end-to-end creative proof.
5. **Execution sequence and resources:** Order work by dependencies and uncertainty reduction, then commit to finish when evidence supports it. Record authorized boundaries, estimated versus known effort/storage/cost, unknown usage, and method checkpoints. Do not reset cumulative effort by changing task names.
6. **Revision and reuse:** Specify how a likely Director revision and the next required shot/episode/use will work without rebuilding everything. Test representative reuse before claiming it. Build for known near-term needs, not hypothetical universal scalability.
7. **Failure and handover policy:** State what falsifies the approach, when local repair should become a strategy review, what constitutes completion, and which decisions require Director authority. Name the next production dependency unlocked by success.

A simple, well-understood correction may satisfy this with a few sentences referencing an existing strategy. A new uncertain subsystem needs a fuller brief and proof. Read-only investigation can establish the brief. Planning should expose decisive choices, not become an excuse to postpone making or reviewing work.

## Repairs and strategy review

A bounded patch is acceptable when its cause is understood, it preserves the chosen design and interfaces, and representative regression checks show that the complete result still works. Artistic shot-specific adjustments are also legitimate when declared, reusable where needed and validated in the intended shot; not every handcrafted correction is a flawed architecture.

Reassess the strategy when the same defect survives three similar variants, a correction repeatedly breaks neighboring behavior, one-view success fails another required view or state, exceptions or manual intervention accumulate, or the next necessary use requires reconstruction. An unsupported foundational assumption triggers review immediately. Existing time checkpoints remain internal reassessments, not new permission requests or permission to exceed hard limits.

At reassessment, compare the total cost and risk of continued patching against a targeted change of representation, construction or workflow. Preserve successful components and failed evidence. Change the method before silently weakening the result; do not rewrite the entire system merely because one interface failed. Record the decision and its evidence. Escalate only a genuine capability/authority blocker or Director-owned consequence, not ordinary technical uncertainty.

## Review and continuation

At the start of a work session, read the current strategy and relevant learnings; identify the current stage, unresolved risk and next proof. Before costly work, ensure the next action advances that strategy. When evidence changes the strategy, update its current decision and retain the superseded rationale as history.

Before handoff, review the integrated result against the declared quality floor and representative downstream use. Technical tests, elapsed effort and improvement over a rejected predecessor do not establish creative acceptance. Once sufficient proof exists, move into production rather than prototype forever. Report progress in terms of usable deliverables and reduced uncertainty, not pass counts.

## Examples across production

- **Story:** establish the episode's dramatic purpose, character choices and causal structure before polishing dialogue. If each scene repair creates a motivation contradiction, reconsider the story structure.
- **Sound:** test voice intelligibility, music and effects together in a representative scene and intended playback context before producing isolated polished stems. Check rights and revision needs early.
- **Animation and wardrobe:** prove coordinated motion, contacts and layered garment behavior together before detailed finish. The [double-wrap contract](../planning/SERIES_01_WARDROBE_TACK_FIT.md#exchange183--double-wrap-construction-and-review-contract) is one application, not the scope of this principle.
- **Editing and delivery:** establish the narrative cut, aspect-ratio/reframing approach and audience-facing formats before rendering everything at final quality. Review a representative finished segment across the required formats.
- **Factory software:** establish authoritative state, interfaces, failure recovery and a thin end-to-end production workflow before multiplying services or provider-specific exceptions. A second representative use tests reuse; class counts do not.
