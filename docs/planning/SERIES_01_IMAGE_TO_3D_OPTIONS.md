## Proposed asset-specific approach

| Asset | Starting route | Acceptance risk |
|---|---|---|
| Her face/head | Fit or generate a candidate from approved front/three-quarter references; compare untextured geometry and textured views at matching angles | A flattering texture can disguise wrong facial structure; newly visible angles must still be her |
| Body, hair, wardrobe | Establish only missing full-body/neutral-pose views with identity fixed; adapt a riggable base or evaluate a generated starting mesh; preserve editable separation where needed | Portraits do not define full body/back details; proposed additions need creative review; fused cloth/limbs/hair may not animate |
| Horse and tack | Preserve existing working rig/motion if visually compatible; adapt surface/look and tack to approved imagery before replacing geometry | Reshaping can invalidate skinning/contact/gait evidence; image-to-mesh alone does not supply trustworthy gallop |
| Foreground scenery/props | Generate or source individual rocks, outcrops, structures and props; arrange in controlled scene geometry | Camera travel and traversable road need consistent geometry, not a pleasing but fused landscape mesh |
| Distant scenery | Consider painted layered backdrops/projections with limited-depth geometry | Only valid over demonstrated camera range; parallax/disocclusion reveals limitations |

Do not feed the horse/rider/landscape hero image into a converter and assume the result contains separate usable assets. The hero frame defines composition/look; isolated consistent asset references define production representations. Single-image generation infers hidden surfaces rather than recovering ground truth. Our front and three-quarter pair helps but is not a complete orthographic turnaround or calibrated reconstruction dataset. Additional views must preserve identity and be checked for consistency, not blindly accumulated.

Mesh geometry is only part of the illustrated language. Painted textures, controlled shading/lighting, edges, palette and compositing still need to match C over time. Image-to-3D is not an automatic illustration-to-movie converter. Check plain shaded geometry and re-lit/rotated textured output so baked shadows or a pasted face do not masquerade as structure.
