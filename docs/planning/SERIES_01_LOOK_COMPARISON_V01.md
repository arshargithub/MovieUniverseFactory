### A — original generation

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ensemble art-direction comparison sheet A for an original history-inspired fictional animated series, The Martials. Provisional concept studies, NOT historical documentation.
Create ONE landscape sheet with THREE equally sized tall 9:16 panels side by side, thin neutral gutters, no text anywhere. Each panel is an independent cinematic frame. Character scale and faces must be readable. All characters are adults. No gore.
LEFT: Muslim Pashtun woman, strong and beautiful through purposeful expression and physical competence, riding a chestnut horse in a spectacular physically plausible gallop toward camera at a slight diagonal on a rocky frontier trail. Flowing layered indigo outer robe and scarf over practical riding trousers, cloth trails clear of reins and hooves. Face visible, focused gaze. Tall trailing dust cloud, rugged mountains, warm rim light, cool blue-gray distance. Show whole horse and rider with ground contact/shadow, reins and stirrups plausible. Not a sexualized costume or decorative princess.
MIDDLE: Jatt Sikh man, black beard and neatly wrapped deep indigo turban, individual expressive face, weathered earth-tone long tunic/coat and practical riding clothes. Mounted on a dark bay horse, almost still, low three-quarter frontal view. Calm intense gaze toward an off-frame confrontation; the horse's poised forward step suggests contained energy and his decision to advance. Stone passage and distant hills, room around silhouette. NOT another gallop, no rearing horse, no gunfire.
RIGHT: Hindu Gurkha man slightly older than the other two, compact athletic build, individually rendered Nepali features without caricature, short dark hair, worn olive-brown practical coat and trousers, no modern uniform insignia. On foot in a narrow stone passage, full body, composed but grounded ready stance with one foot subtly advanced. An adult traveler behind him is getting clear, smaller and secondary. Threat remains offscreen ahead. Human composure before explosive close-quarters action; formidable rather than elderly, passive or comic. No attack rendered, no blood.
STYLE A: painterly cinematic 3D realism. Believable human and equine anatomy, sculpted three-dimensional volumes, selective brush-textured surfaces, expressive faces, rich material separation of cloth/leather/stone. Designed shadows and atmospheric depth, not photorealistic pores, not glossy game characters. Earth and stone with indigo, selective golden light, not all sepia. Mature graphic-novel sensibility balanced with warmth and emotional range.
Constraints: three consistent world designs, no specific real year, no fantasy armor, no supernatural effects, no modern guns or vehicles, no ethnic superpowers or religious-symbol decoration. All faces/clothes are provisional. Strong vertical compositions, equal visual importance of all three leads. Only three panels; no captions, letters, logos or watermarks.
