# Production contracts, finite feasibility and bounded context — proposal392

Status: **PROPOSED, NOT ADOPTED OR DEPLOYED.** Director392 requested critical
analysis and a framework. This document does not extend a production allowance,
change accepted art, activate new controller rules or authorize provider calls.
Implement as extensions to the existing332/330/350/386 controller and canonical
database, not a second orchestration system.

## 1. What determinism can and cannot mean

Reasoning proposes requirements, representations, diagnoses and visual judgments.
Code validates the required contracts, measurements, evidence coverage, limits
and transitions. Freeze a proposed contract before the attempt; do not move the
threshold after seeing the result. Make every decision reproducible from its
versioned inputs and recorded assessments.

A deterministic transition is not a deterministic truth oracle. A model can
incorrectly assert that a surface looks natural. Turning that assertion into a
number and comparing it with a threshold does not make it objective. Keep four
field types distinct:

| Type | Example | What code establishes |
|---|---|---|
|Measurement|New-region intersections at pose339; actual motion deviation|Value meets the frozen, calibrated comparator on the bound artifact.|
|Evidence|Required front/side/reverse and phase coverage|Exact required cells exist, belong to this version/pose/profile, and received the permitted review modality.|
|Judgment|Chest has supported connected anatomy; same-film coherence|An authorized assessor gave a source-bound decision against anchored criteria. The judgment itself remains fallible.|
|Authority|Director accepted a performance or approved a tradeoff|The bound decision has the required scope/provenance. A machine cannot authenticate the semantic meaning of arbitrary human prose.|

Do not average these into a quality score. Every critical criterion must pass;
an UNKNOWN is not a pass, and many technical passes cannot offset one visible
structural failure. Director313 retains full-speed/audio judgment. The agent
cannot fill unavailable modalities with invented ratings.

Controlled-entry guarantees remain limited to the registered router/bridge.
Raw chat tools are not OS-intercepted. Existing instructions prohibit bypass;
absolute enforcement would require a separately qualified restricted-tool broker.
This proposal does not claim that infrastructure exists.

## 2. Stable identities and the two work lanes

Track `deliverable_id → capability_goal_id → strategy_family_id → micro_unit_id
→ attempt_id`. The canonical production task remains the parent. IDs and effort
survive prompts, model switches, replans, helper scripts and subdivisions.

- **Production lane:** qualify a finite approach and then deliver usable footage.
  A failed qualification is a practical stop, not permission for indefinite R&D.
- **Capability R&D lane:** Director explicitly chooses a bounded experiment for
  learning, with a falsifiable hypothesis and ceiling. It must not be forecast
  as a nearly ready production deliverable or silently consume production scope.
- Read-only research, display, audits and engineering qualification have tailored
  lightweight contracts. They always route, but do not require the artistic
  production pipeline. Engineering work still consumes its enclosing authorized
  resource/correction limits; it is not a free retry lane.

Earlier Director instructions prioritized agent-only feasibility over speed.
That authorized exploration. It did not establish a practical production method.
Make that distinction explicit going forward, rather than retrospectively calling
all authorized discovery waste or allowing it to continue without a stopping test.

## 3. Minimum contract carried across existing stages

| Existing stage | Required output | Deterministic admission condition |
|---|---|---|
|Router|Actual operations, work lane, original goal/task, authority and affected interfaces|Known classification; no unmapped effects; original counters/retirements loaded.|
|Grounding|Applicable adopted rules, Director intent/preferences, accepted and rejected controls, references with roles, rights/inputs, invariant ownership|All required source IDs covered; unknown/conflicting authority reconciled; no rights/spend inference.|
|Derivation|Audience-visible critical features and optional details, expert-lens derivation, tests and framing/phase requirements|Every critical goal maps to a test and evidence cell; no omitted stated goal, orphan criterion or missing reference-role decision.|
|Strategy|Chosen family, alternatives, factual dependencies, earliest actual artifact, finite budgets, stop predicates and protected proofs|No retired family without exception; finite parent envelope; early proof step exists in the executable graph.|
|Challenge|Trace actual handler operations to that earliest proof; compare predicted failure/success, constraints and alternatives|No build requiring an unqualified form can precede its form proof; no cache requiring an untested pose can precede its pose proof; all prerequisite kinds match.|
|Execution|Frozen input/handler versions, reserved attempt, exact outputs, measured resources and technical observations|Registered handler only; resources/counters reserved before launch; at-most-once, one heavy lease and no silent retry.|
|Review|Typed measured results and judgments; exact evidence matrix, regressions, feature closure and hypothesis disposition|All required cells covered; no image-review label without an image; no critical known failure in Director-ready media.|
|Decision/retro|Continue, repair, retire family, retire capability for production, queue Director review, or request a material tradeoff|One deterministic decision function using frozen assessments/counters; no discretionary bypass in prose.|

The existing planning substages remain. Deterministic stages do not need extra
LLM sessions, and expert lenses are not a studio org chart. Share one canonical
contract; downstream stages reference it and its changed fields rather than
repeating increasingly large summaries.

Required criterion fields:

```
id, parent_goal, source_and_role, criticality, kind,
assessor_and_available_modality, evidence_cells,
metric_definition_or_judgment_anchor, comparator, threshold,
calibration_and_negative_control, coordinate_space,
pose_or_time_window, camera_and_render_profile, input_version,
invariant_owner, dependencies, invalidation_conditions
```

Separate Director-owned outcomes, true consumer dependencies, agent-owned method
choices and calibrated numerical tolerances. An exact internal vertex/bone freeze
cannot silently become a creative requirement. A technical guard cannot be
widened after failure merely to admit a candidate.

## 4. Quantification that informs the right decisions

Maintain a compact feature ledger. For the horse: supported chest silhouette,
continuous lower-neck transition, coherent shoulder/foreleg roots, acceptable
raised deformation, and moving/contact consistency. Each has the required
pose/view cells. A cell passes only against its fixed criterion; a feature closes
only when its complete required set passes and prior protected results survive.

| Metric/signal | Definition | Use |
|---|---|---|
|Native feature closure|Newly satisfied critical feature/cell IDs on the same contract|Primary evidence of production progress. No credit from reducing unrelated numerical error.|
|Proof retention|Previously passing critical IDs still pass under changed interfaces|Any unapproved critical regression blocks dependent work and requires repair/rollback within remaining authority.|
|Visual stagnation|Comparable native candidate attempts with no new critical visual closure|Triggers finite family retirement below; a smaller still-failing bulge does not reset it.|
|Early-evidence delay|Charged slots after qualified execution path with no required representative visual artifact|Stops a production method that is consuming effort without exposing its main risk.|
|Family/capability effort|Every charged slot and reserved model/native/provider attempt, grouped under stable parents|Hard limits; renaming, micro-task splitting or model switches do not reset them.|
|False passes/rejections|Known positive/negative controls plus Director disagreement on exact outputs|Calibrate review; missing positive controls make accuracy unknown, not100%.|
|Director escapes/handoffs|Known defects first identified by Director; unscheduled approval/handoff count and reasons|Measure actual reduction in avoidable Director work. Never optimize by withholding all output forever.|
|Owned context bytes|Serialized stage capsule and required source excerpt sizes|Govern what the Factory controls. Never convert bytes or account percentages into token claims.|
|Administrative work|Typed work intervals, source assembly, validation, native occupancy, active inference when actually measured|Identify overhead. Uninstrumented time remains unknown; union overlaps and exclude verified Director idle.|

Optional anchored ordinal judgments can help diagnosis, but not unlock a gate in
place of a binary critical criterion. Freeze anchors and the assessor, retain
counterexamples and calibration errors, and label them subjective. Quantitative
proxies are admitted only within tested sensitivity/visibility conditions.

Measurements are comparable only if metric, affected region/topology conditions,
pose/coordinate space and profile are comparable. Zero crossings in261rest space
cannot be presented as repairing473crossings in a different posed surface.
Likewise nonnegative weights summing to one do not establish anatomical binding.

## 5. Finite stop policy — proposed initial settings

The numbers below are **decision-policy defaults, not measured universal optima**.
Director adoption is needed. Set a profile before work and tune it prospectively,
not after failure. A stricter original allowance always wins. These are attempt
bounds, not arbitrary30/60minute deadlines; long valid renders are not evidence
that an artistic method failed.

| Scope | Proposed default | On reaching the limit without its proof |
|---|---|---|
|Known execution path, first representative visual proof|Within2charged slots|Stop family as `QUALIFICATION_NOT_DEMONSTRATED`; no more build/detail first.|
|An active strategy family|At most6charged correction slots total|Retire that family for this goal/envelope if qualification is absent.|
|Visual stagnation within a family|3comparable native visual candidates without new critical visual closure|Retire family earlier, even if slots remain.|
|Unqualified native/toolchain bring-up|Separate engineering profile, at most12charged slots, zero/control + real representative artifact required|Stop as implementation unqualified, not a proof of artistic impossibility.|
|Capability qualification on an already qualified path|At most2materially distinct families, at most12totalcharged slots|Retire capability approach for this production goal/envelope if no integrated qualification.|
|Capability requiring engineering bring-up too|Same family caps plus engineering cap, at most24totalcharged slots|Stop the production investment; no budget multiplication across children.|
|A known identical technical error after a purported repair|Second recurrence|Suspend and reassess implementation; all spent slots remain counted.|
|Resource/authority exhaustion, critical source drift, forbidden rights/tool use|Immediate|Block; continue only independently authorized work.|

One material family falsifier can retire it before any numerical cap. A positive
partial result may be retained, but does not erase stagnation elsewhere or give
another six slots. A qualified capability can move into a separately budgeted
production/finishing package, still within a finite Director-owned deliverable
envelope. Inventing a new micro-unit cannot mint a new qualification budget.

Engineering qualification is available only for an objectively missing supported
operation, failed zero/transport/control test or unestablished source ownership.
An unattractive chest is not an engineering-lane admission reason. Generating new
anatomical target geometry/weights counts as construction-family effort even when
it runs in NumPy and produces no image. The engineering pool is shared by the
whole capability, not renewed for every new handler. Select the profile before
the attempt; a post-failure lane/profile change needs explicit reconciliation and
cannot refund effort. Default profiles are chosen by task class, so the Director
does not approve routine per-unit numerical tuning. R&D extensions and material
quality/resource tradeoffs remain Director decisions.

Why distinguish engineering bring-up? A naive two-cycle first-image rule applied
from scratch would have stopped the eventually accepted preparation58–66: native
records show no images until66, after technical/control work and failures.
Therefore that naive universal rule is **rejected**. The proposed early-image
limit applies after the execution path is qualified; unsupported authoring gets
the finite engineering lane instead. This is a known-history sensitivity check,
not proof that these new defaults are optimal or would predict every success.

An exhausted or retired production family is not reactivated by “ten more cycles”
alone. Require an explicit, source-bound exception naming its retired status,
new evidence/question, exact permitted experiment and finite ceiling. Record it
as R&D or terminal salvage, not a counter reset or a production-success forecast.
Director choices can override a proposed policy once adopted, but not by erasing
history, falsifying acceptance or inferring spend/publication authority.

A charged slot means the existing canonical correction reservation, including
an attempt that fails in planning, implementation or transport; failed work is not
free. Keep separate counts for actual visual candidates, native jobs, LLM calls
and provider charges. Do not call all of these “turns.” A research/qualification
package has a predeclared count/reservation profile too. Resource budgets are
vectors: available correction slots do not imply spend or live model-call authority.

Predeclare critical features at a meaningful, audience-visible scale. Adding a
trivial evidence cell or changing the checklist cannot reset stagnation. Any
contract revision retains its predecessor, effort and original unmet outcome.
Qualified finishing packages also need finite acceptance scope and a parent
reservation; the word “qualified” is not an unlimited polish budget.

## 6. Deterministic transition order

1. Reject missing scope/authority, stale inputs, unsupported tool/model use or
   unreserved resources. External blocks are not anatomical failure evidence.
2. Before any new work, enforce root-goal/family retirement and all cumulative caps. A valid bounded
   exception authorizes only its exact operations/count, not a broad restart.
3. Validate stage prerequisites and evidence kinds. If an early prerequisite is
   missing, the next permitted operation produces it; dependent fitting/detail
   cannot launch. Engineering qualification is allowed only in its bounded lane.
4. Reject incomplete/inconsistent evidence or known critical regressions. Route
   repair to the affected stage within remaining authority; do not replan forever.
5. Apply material falsifier, early-evidence and stagnation retirement predicates.
   If a comparable alternative is already authorized and within the parent
   envelope, the agent may switch internally. Otherwise produce a Director
   tradeoff decision: new source/budget, altered goal/framing, bounded R&D, or defer.
6. If all required agent-scope criteria pass, queue the agreed Director review;
   full-speed/audio remain human-pending under313. No known defect waiver.
7. Otherwise continue the admissible next operation. Completion, a retired/exhausted
   route with no authorized alternative, or a material Director choice are the
   handover boundaries. Routine setup, tests and partial improvement are not.

Evaluate a completed, correctly reserved result before rejecting *further* work
at a ceiling: success on the last allowed slot may proceed to review. This does
not grant another slot or excuse missing checks. In a terminal exception, an
already complete proof may close successfully; a valid anatomical failure, a new
variant or an unfinished proof at the cap closes unsuccessfully.

`RETIRED_FOR_PRODUCTION` means the bounded claim failed: this method has not
delivered the contracted proof with these goals/resources/effort. It does not
claim no future agent/tool could ever solve anatomy. That universal proposition
is irrelevant to allocating the next production effort.

## 7. Strategy identity and protection against gaming

Register the family using representation, geometry provenance, deformation
mechanism, frozen visual goal and constraints. Changing a coefficient, name,
camera label or model does not create a new family. A topology replacement is a
material change, but still spends the original capability envelope. Semantic
classification needs a challenged judgment; a hash cannot prove novelty.
Unknown classification blocks reconciliation. The parent cap applies regardless
of a mistaken subfamily label, limiting this loophole.

A changed quality target or accepted action requires a versioned Director decision.
Record the original goal as unmet/superseded, not retroactively passed. Publish
the tradeoff: what audience effect survives, what is sacrificed and what becomes
unsupported. Optional unseen detail may be scoped out; visible broken structure
cannot be relabeled polish. A deliberately different shot/representation needs
its own honest proof, not a covert clothing/camera concealment waiver.

## 8. Context/admin reduction without loss

Use three layers, with the existing local catalog/SQLite rather than a new vector
service or graph server:

1. **Mandatory active contract:** current adopted rules/Director decisions,
   authority/retirement/resource limits, active critical features, relevant
   failures, latest plan/proof and pending decisions. Nothing here is selected
   solely by semantic-search ranking.
2. **Typed dependency/provenance graph:** immutable manifests contain the complete
   executable dependency closure and history links. Code verifies full bindings;
   the LLM receives direct inputs, changed interfaces and required excerpts.
   Search discovers context; it cannot confer authority or qualification.
3. **Immutable cold history:** full prior plans, failed media, raw events, code
   versions and exact source excerpts remain available and auditable. No deletion,
   summarization-as-replacement or mass repinning.

Proposed owned stage-capsule target: **24KiB** of dynamic task payload, excluding
the mandatory host/core instructions. This is a design target to test, not a
promise about the app's total context or a reason to drop required content.
Keep the existing48,000-byte verified-source and60,000-character retrospective
limits unchanged initially. Overflow requires explicit scope/source reconciliation;
no silent truncation. Do not load1,019bindings into prose when one verified
manifest plus actual direct dependencies suffices; transitive verification remains.

Give each substantive attempt one shared experiment/response body and typed
member-finding outcomes. Preserve all per-finding coverage and original event
identities. A batch transaction/checkpointed projection can avoid replaying and
copying the same history for each of nine similar responses. Never merge away
different statuses, unresolved flags, authority or evidence requirements.

Mutable release-status prose should be a routing/reporting view, not an immutable
worker input. Move current authority into source-bound, versioned decision records
and consume an audited relevant-facts projection. Preserve whole-source versions
for provenance. A changed Director decision invalidates consumers; a cosmetic
exchange-counter update should not invalidate unrelated native evidence. Existing
whole-file checks remain until that new boundary is equivalence-tested; do not
silently exclude status today.

Do not promise a percentage token saving. Measure owned request bytes, unique
source loads, projection/validation time, applicable-rule recall, Director escapes
and time per qualified output. Actual prompt/token savings remain unknown until
observed. Conversation history and host instructions are not removed by shrinking
our local payload. A future fresh-session runtime needs its own qualification;
this does not renew325calls or require separate LLMs per stage.

## 9. Safe migration and acceptance

Implement in separate, reversible increments after adoption:

1. Add read-only feature/family/cost projections and compact capsules alongside
   existing guards. No native admission behavior changes first.
2. Replay known failure and successful micro-motion histories; explicitly retain
   semantic false-PASS controls and hindsight limitations. Test legitimate R&D,
   technical bring-up, unavailable modalities and incomplete records too.
3. Introduce typed stage-proof requirements and finite qualification/retirement
   predicates under a versioned policy, with explicit original-task migration.
   No old run is relabeled as violating a policy that did not exist then.
4. Only afterward batch events/cache projections, proving equivalence with full
   replay before making the optimized projection authoritative.
5. Pilot on one bounded real unit after zero/technical controls. Do not claim
   production benefits from mocked replays or an offline decision function.

Required no-loss checks:100%of the defined mandatory source/decision IDs, all
open findings, original/shared budgets, retired families, pending jobs and accepted
proof conditions survive restart/migration; zero verdict differences on preserved
fixtures between full and incremental projection; deliberately remove each
critical field/source/edge and require a block. Require actual rights/spend/model
and unsupported-native negative tests. Calibrate the positive/negative art controls
separately; do not call structural tests perceptual validation.

Roll out only at an idle/heavy-job boundary, preserve the old database/export and
old projection, then test crash/restart and rollback. Rollback cannot restore spent
budgets, erase retirements, reopen revoked permissions or permit old code to ignore
new hard constraints; unsupported old versions become read-only/blocked. Do not
push/upload/delete snapshots without their existing explicit authority.

Over-strict gates can stop a method that would eventually succeed. The bounded
R&D lane preserves that choice transparently. False visual judgments can still
pass a syntactically valid contract. Unmapped historical decisions remain gaps.
These are retained limitations, not problems hidden by an enforcement percentage.
