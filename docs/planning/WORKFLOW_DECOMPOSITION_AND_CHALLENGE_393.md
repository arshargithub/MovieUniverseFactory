## A scope/diagnosis contract at admission and at triggered retros

Use one compact extension inside the existing work-unit/strategy contract:

| Field | Required meaning and evidence |
|---|---|
|Primary question|One answer that will change the next method/production decision.|
|Audience-visible proof|Named meaningful feature(s), context, references/roles and the exact pose/view/phase needed to judge the answer.|
|Unknowns and prerequisites|Which target, source, mechanism, integration and observation questions remain unresolved; bind actual proof IDs rather than calling an unqualified baseline accepted.|
|Change set|Actual operations/parameters and touched domains; one controlled causal contrast or a justified inseparable mechanism bundle. Several coordinated edits are not automatically several tasks.|
|Outcome branches|Distinct predicted observations and the next action for each; a failed test must narrow a cause, stop a family or route repair.|
|Scope alternative|A concrete smaller candidate proof and why it is sufficient/insufficient, including coupled context and what would be frozen legitimately.|
|Interfaces and invariants|What neighboring form/contact/performance must remain visible; whether each freeze is Director-owned, consumer-required, agent-chosen or numerical.|
|Lineage and reservation|Stable original goal/family, shared effort and remaining authority; no child budget reset.|

The model reasons about these attributes. Code verifies their presence/types,
source/proof conditions, operation bindings, dependency order and allowed next
transition. The model's coupling/causality judgment remains fallible and must be
challenged against concrete evidence. A typed edge is not proof the edge is true.

### Decision sequence

1. Enforce existing authority and any adopted retirement/resource limits first.
   Decomposition cannot revive an exhausted family.
2. Distinguish missing reference/observability, technical failure, incompatible
   constraints, wrong method/representation and excessive scope. An anatomical
   failure does not automatically imply “split further.”
3. If a required upstream proof is unqualified, the next operation must produce
   that proof or test its feasibility. Dependent construction remains blocked.
4. If outcomes are independently judgeable under valid inputs, split into ordered
   proof stages/children with explicit dependencies. Keep the parent final goal.
5. If an anatomical/contact boundary is inseparable, keep or merge that coupled
   context, but isolate the next causal contrast and earliest revealing pose.
   A merge does not authorize unrelated finish, actors or a full-length render.
6. If no meaningful smaller proof exists and the current method cannot achieve
   the coupled result under its envelope, change method or retire. Do not fragment
   muscles/vertices to manufacture progress.

Keep/split/merge is therefore the result of diagnosis, not the automatic response
to a failure count. Determinism governs when reassessment is mandatory and what
evidence/execution follows; it cannot independently discover anatomical causality.

### Mandatory triggers proposed

| Observed signal | Required decision before more dependent execution |
|---|---|
|Admission of a new micro-unit or material method change|Assess question, prerequisites, scope alternatives and actual first operation.|
|Missing target-form proof before fitting/binding/finish|Immediate ordered-proof hold; target form is next, not dense fitting.|
|One attempt changes independently testable form and deformation mechanisms without a discriminating control|Immediate causal-scope hold; split contrasts or justify/test their necessary coupling.|
|Second ineffective intervention on the same stable critical feature across admitted responses|Mandatory scope/method/constraint reassessment; do not reset this count on another response, micro-name or model.|
|Repair repeatedly breaks a neighboring proof|Immediate interface review; second distinct recurrence requires explicit keep/merge/representation decision.|
|Failure cannot be localized from required evidence|Evidence/observability repair before more anatomical variants; not an automatic task split.|
|Contradictory proof or impossible source/freeze assumptions|Immediate contract/constraint reconciliation; no “keep because coupled” shortcut.|
|Family/goal limit reached|Retire or invoke an explicitly approved finite exception; splitting cannot replenish resources.|

These additions refine the existing second-warning/third-hold policy; they remain
proposals. Count distinct interventions/candidates once, not views, callbacks or
per-finding response rows. Technical non-results still consume total effort but
do not become fake visual failures. Uncertain comparability cannot be quietly
discarded: retain uncertainty and enforce the independent parent ceiling.

A repeated KEEP needs a new evidence-backed discriminator and predicted branches,
not the same “anatomically connected” explanation. It spends existing authority;
it does not override stagnation/retirement. Missing evidence routes to a bounded
diagnostic, not a new routine Director gate. Material goals/resources stay Director-owned.

## Fresh critique of framework392

### 1. Reasoned booleans must not masquerade as authoritative state

The34-case illustration tested selected branch behavior, not the full contract.
Fresh adversarial inputs show it can accept a string “false” as truthy authority,
accept a caller-declared engineering lane, and allow caller-supplied zero counters.
These are limitations of an explicitly non-production prototype, not demonstrated
exploits in the existing native controller. They show that adoption needs strict
types and counters/authority/proof status derived from canonical versioned events,
not a caller's `contract_complete` or `lineage_complete` assertion.

### 2. Contradiction veto must precede aggregate success

The illustration can queue an overall PASS alongside an explicit terminal anatomy
FAIL or falsified family, and can miss absent image evidence if an art-disposition
label is omitted. Derive aggregate readiness from criterion-level results;
explicit FAIL/UNKNOWN, stale evidence and inconsistent assertions block first.
Evidence requirements come from task/contract type, never optional caller labels.
Last-slot success is allowed only for a complete internally consistent proof.

`results/workflow393/COUNTEREXAMPLES.json` preserves eight adversarial scenarios,
including missing integrated state and regression accounting. Not all are logic
bugs; some expose intentionally omitted integration. None qualifies replacement
production logic or a perceptual oracle.392's34/34 must not be called robustness.

### 3. Default cycle thresholds are not calibrated production policy

The2/3/6/12/24numbers are candidates for shadow testing, not established settings.
Accepted prep58–66already exposes false-stop risk. Use several known successes,
failures and engineering bring-ups, retain hindsight limitations, then prospectively
pilot within an explicit finite allowance. Do not tune every threshold to admit
every historical success or claim a universal optimum from one horse.

Counters must be operation/family/goal-specific and bounded independently of
uncertain semantic labels. Profile choice and transitions bind before launch;
an agent cannot expand12to24by toggling `needs_engineering`. A useful technical
discovery is recorded separately from visual closure and never resets root effort.

### 4. “First image” and feature counts can be gamed

A meaningless render is not representative proof. Require the intended failure
condition, region, revealing pose/framing and version. Adding trivial cells or
closing easy subchecks cannot reset the unresolved critical-feature trend.
Track retained critical feature closure, not number of child tasks passed.

The existing final appearance contract requires at least3poses/3views. An early
single-pose proof must be recorded as a subproof, not a final unit PASS, unless a
reviewed migration changes that schema. Keep lightweight early rejection separate
from complete final coverage; otherwise decomposition either becomes expensive
or silently weakens the existing review floor.

### 5. Constraint and human-review bottlenecks need explicit accounting

Before blaming scope or model capacity, identify any incompatible constraints and
their owners. Agent-imposed internal freezes are revisable; accepted visible
motion/identity is not silently negotiable. A smaller unit with an invalid input
or impossible freeze can still fail indefinitely.

Internal vetoes without qualified outputs are not success. Measure false rejections
on known positives, qualified subproof retention, time/resources per usable result
and Director queue age. Preserve human AV boundaries; do not add unplanned human
review to every micro-stage or claim static frames certify motion.

### 6. Cycle counts alone do not establish economic efficiency

Keep corrections, actual visual candidates, native jobs, LLM calls and measured
active/administrative time separate. Fixed counts prevent infinity but do not make
two costly attempts cheaper than six cheap ones. Where usage is unavailable, state
unknown and use known reserved-call/job/byte budgets without inventing dollar
or token estimates. No new arbitrary elapsed-time cutoff is imposed.

### 7. The process can itself become the failure mode

Prefer strengthening existing work-unit/retro projections, not adding a new stage,
service, LLM call, manual form or repeated narrative per finding. Store full cold
history; active packets contain verified current obligations and compact deltas.
Measure packet assembly/replay overhead and mandatory-context recall. A24KiBtarget
cannot justify dropping an obligation or claiming the host conversation shrank.

No-loss validation must check effective conditions and graph edges, not ID presence
alone: a retained proof ID with changed authority/pose/status is still a loss.
Missing/changed dependency, negative status, retirement, budget, pending job and
accepted-proof conditions must fail the optimized replay as they do full replay.

### 8. The optional two-cycle graft probe needs a feasibility precondition

392correctly labels it a terminal exception, not another sculpt loop. But the
native handler is drafted and unregistered; two cycles are not a measured estimate
of integration effort. Before recommending a grant, a bounded read-only preflight
must enumerate handler qualification, operations, evidence, jobs and resources.
If these cannot credibly fit the finite exception, skip it and pursue the next
representation. Do not fund unbounded engineering before the nominal two cycles.
No such grant, preflight construction or production dispatch is authorized here.
