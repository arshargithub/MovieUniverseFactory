## Exact prompts

### B

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate from scratch an original ensemble visual development sheet for a mature animated action-adventure. THREE equal vertical 9:16 panels arranged side by side with thin plain gutters. No text. This is direction B: DESIGNED SCULPTURAL 3D ANIMATION, not live action with a filter. All figures clearly animated designs with deliberately stylized individual proportions and large expressive face planes, strong brows, simplified noses and hair/beard masses, graphic hands, elongated elegant action lines. Sophisticated adult animation, not chibi, not caricature of ethnic traits. Three-dimensional sculpted geometry, broad matte material surfaces, decisive light/shadow, very limited microtexture. Think hand-designed animation maquettes brought into a cinematic world, NOT real actors, NOT pores, NOT photo-derived concept art, NOT painterly filter.
World: reimagined northern subcontinental frontier inspired by nineteenth-century terrain/cultures, no precise real year. Warm stone and cool indigo, selective rust accents, expressive designed environments with large geometric cliff masses and negative space. No generic magical symbols.
LEFT: adult Muslim Pashtun female lead in spectacular gallop on a chestnut horse. A strong flowing indigo robe/scarf silhouette over practical riding layers, cloth safely clear of tack. Focused face visible. Wide three-quarter dynamic view of whole horse and rider; diagonal forward motion; sculpted sweeping dust shapes. Horse has stylized elegant long curves, strong chest and believable limb structure, not a toy. Clothes described by three or four bold folds rather than hundreds. Woman reads powerful and intentional, not princess or sexualized.
CENTER: adult Jatt Sikh man with indigo turban and full beard, mounted on a dark bay horse almost still at an offscreen confrontation. Strong tapered silhouette, deliberately designed expressive face, earth-toned long coat reduced to readable shape masses. Low-angle mounted presence with contained energy, NOT gallop or rearing. Look of assessment before commitment. Architecture as angular tall masses frames horse/rider.
RIGHT: slightly older adult Hindu Gurkha man, compact athletic build, short hair, individually designed Nepali face without ethnic exaggeration. Practical olive-brown clothes, feet planted in narrow stone passage, quiet intense composure before sudden close-quarters force. An adult traveler behind him moves to safety. Guarding figure occupies foreground with powerful readable pose and expressive hands. No blood or attack shown.
Every panel must look built from deliberately stylized 3D characters and worlds, including faces, body proportions and horses. No modern firearms or vehicles, fantasy armor, religious decoration, lettering or logos. Preserve each character's human dignity and equal visual importance. Costumes/faces are exploratory, not historical claims. New composition allowed to express the style. No image references.

### C

Use case: illustration-story / stylized-concept. Create a radically distinctive original art-direction sheet for a mature action-adventure series: THREE equal tall 9:16 panels side by side with narrow plain gutters, no text. Direction C: DRAWN AND PAINTED GRAPHIC STORYTELLING WITH LAYERED 2.5D DEPTH. This must look authored as illustration from the first line, not a realistic image painted over. Expressive variable-width ink contours, deliberately elongated elegant figures and compressed spaces, flat gouache color masses, selective exposed paper, silhouettes and negative space. Layered overlapping planes rather than photographic lens perspective. Faces drawn with a few precise expressive strokes, no skin texture or modeled photoreal faces. Material differences expressed through marks and shape rather than PBR. Rich indigo, terracotta, warm ivory and charcoal, controlled accent colors, mature not nursery-storybook, not anime, not photorealistic, not a 3D render. Preserve readable action and human character.
World: openly reimagined northern subcontinental frontier, historically inspired cultures and imperial setting but not documentary or exact year. No pseudo-cultural symbols, religious decoration or invented calligraphy.
LEFT: Muslim Pashtun adult woman with purposeful gaze and flowing indigo robe/scarf over practical riding layers, horse galloping dramatically on trail with trailing dust. Use a sweeping curved line of action, side/three-quarter profile that captures whole chestnut horse and rider. Robe becomes one or two flowing drawn shapes; dust broad ivory sweeps, mountain shapes overlap upward. Face visible, strong purposeful person not decorative silhouette. Believable riding mechanics despite drawn exaggeration.
CENTER: adult Jatt Sikh warrior with turban and full beard on dark bay horse, mounted confrontation. Nearly still, looking toward an off-frame obstacle with resolve. Upright vertical silhouette, quiet space around him, tall flattened cliff/architecture planes; earth-tone coat with sparse expressive folds. NOT another gallop or charge. Presence and decision, not invulnerability.
RIGHT: adult Hindu Gurkha slightly older, compact athletic frame, short hair, individual Nepali face drawn without ethnic caricature. Composed on foot guarding a narrow stone passage, one advanced foot, weight ready to shift. Adult traveler behind him gets clear. Strong foreground silhouette framed by angular doorway and a pale strip of light. Imminent concentrated force and protection, no blood or attack shown.
All three scenes must have fully illustrated character construction, stylized horses, authored spatial arrangements, expressive outlines and selective detail from one coherent drawing language. No photographic textures, CGI rendering, detailed pores, ornate fantasy armor, modern guns/vehicles, logos, titles, labels or speech bubbles. Keep three protagonists equally important. Composition differs freely to serve this illustrated language. No image references.
