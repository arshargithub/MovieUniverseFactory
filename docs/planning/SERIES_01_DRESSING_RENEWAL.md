## Learning

Preserve donor normal/material channels until each is deliberately assessed; replacing a material can discard useful detail. But neither added surface detail nor mathematically continuous topology establishes correct drape. Small mathematical fold adjustments are not a substitute for an authored/simulated garment. Stop at the three-variant checkpoint when the silhouette remains wrong. Licensing and preview access must be verified before recommending a paid replacement.
