## Decision and deliverables

Question: can our existing structured foundation carry a readable opening, while providing a credible route to the selected woman's identity and illustrated C look? A proxy camera success alone cannot answer the whole question.

Target deliverables for this pilot:

1. One nine-second vertical continuous opening preview, silent, inexpensive rendering, same horse traveling continuously on a broad road. No roadblock, branch or battle. Placeholder rider, if required, visibly labeled; do not present it as her accepted representation.
2. One representative late-three-quarter frame from this **same production setup**, compared with approved portrait pair B and illustrated C. It must disclose whether it is a rendered persistent representation, a repeatable composite, or merely an art target. A pasted/generated attractive face with no repeatable production path does not pass identity feasibility. Do not commission another portrait-variant campaign.
3. One bounded revision of camera timing/framing, if requested, with horse motion and scene preserved. Save editable scene/control settings and report interventions, preservation evidence, render/engineering time, storage and exact API spend. A code-assisted correction is disclosed, not mislabeled parameter-only operation.

These are target outputs, not a promise that a production-ready character can be created within the first pass. If asset readiness makes item 2 infeasible, report that dependency early rather than quietly changing the pilot into camera-only success.

## First-pass bounds and stop conditions

Propose authorizing **only the first 60 net elapsed minutes**, including setup, tests, rendering and packaging; exclude Director/next-prompt waits, not unattended render time. This is a hard stop, not a promise of all target outputs. At 20 net minutes reassess asset/reuse feasibility. If there is no credible identity/look path within the remaining allowance, stop with the concrete missing representation and a separately estimated next step; do not spend the remainder polishing a known camera capability just to deliver a clip.

- USD 0 new paid API calls, purchases or cloud rendering in this first pass. Earlier unused preview or demonstrator allowance is not renewed spend authority.
- One worker; target 360×640 or 540×960 at 24 fps. Choose resolution from measured cost before full output; low-cost staging is not an art-quality verdict. One base candidate and at most one scoped revision within the same allowance, not multiple aesthetic variants.
- Maximum 1 GiB new outputs and maintain at least 5 GiB free on each relevant workspace/system volume; do not delete evidence to meet limits. If source duplication/restoration would exceed the cap, stop and propose scoped reuse.
- No new harness, general renderer qualification, cloth/weather simulation, face sculpting from scratch, higher-resolution final campaign, Runway reroll or provider tournament.
- Engineering token use remains unknown unless supported per-task telemetry is available. Capture lifecycle prospectively on execution, render job timings, measurable call/test counts and coverage gaps. Do not infer tokens from elapsed time or account allowance.

The matched-look representation may require a separately approved asset investment. This proposal deliberately exposes that dependency instead of hiding it behind a flattering still or promising a finished recurring character in one hour.

## Acceptance and next decision

Review complete playback at intended speed. Control passes only if approach/side/reveal are readable, horse travel remains energetic and continuous, screen direction is coherent, and the bounded camera change preserves protected motion/state. Technical checks cover decode/duration, frame sequence, scene reopen and relevant contact/preservation evidence, not a broad repeated suite. Director judgments remain separate from machine checks.

Look/identity passes only with recognizably the approved adult woman, simple indigo wardrobe/head covering, fuller face/strong jaw, self-possession and the textured illustrated C visual language—not generic beauty or generic smooth toon shading. One matched frame is a prerequisite, **not** proof of moving or multi-angle identity. Validate its temporal continuation before production scale.

If control succeeds but identity/look is absent, report **partial / YELLOW**, not a successful production pilot. Next decision is representation acquisition/adaptation, not more camera work. If both have a credible basis, propose a short moving direct-render result; only then consider separately priced reference-guided generative finishing if direct output misses the look. Hybrid must preserve identity, camera and motion in observed output, not merely promise it. Full actual-series and release gates remain open.
