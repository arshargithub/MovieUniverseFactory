# Shot / beat performance and believability brief

Adopted operating requirement at Director exchange249, 2026-10-13. Extends the existing audience brief, motion brief and production strategy; not another Director approval form or a new production department. Applies to every planned shot and its materially distinct beats. Group shared requirements once, then record beat-specific differences. A static insert can have a short brief with justified not-applicable lenses.

## Responsibility: expertise lenses, one integrated result

The executing agent owns a shot-supervision function: reason from the intended audience experience, apply the relevant expertise, resolve conflicting requirements and verify the whole result. Do not wait for the Director to identify technical failures. The Director supplies intent, material creative choices and acceptance, not joint-placement, simulation or deformation diagnoses.

Apply these lenses to the same versioned shot artifact, not a mandatory chain of agents:

- **Story, performance and audience:** what changes for viewers, character intention, recognizable identity, emotional legibility, attention and larger-than-life signature moment.
- **Animation and physical action:** support, balance, effort, momentum, coordinated joints, weight transfer, impact and recovery. Skilled control is not rigid stillness.
- **Anatomy, rigging and contact:** articulation pivots, deforming volume, skin/cloth boundaries, fingers and grip, feet/props/partners, reach and contact ownership.
- **Character effects and environmental response:** hair, mane, tail, cloth, straps, dust/debris; supports, lag, inertia, gravity, forcing and collisions must tell a compatible physical story.
- **Camera, look and editorial:** what the framing actually exposes, continuity and causal geography, silhouette, palette/lighting/material readability, pace and exposure duration.
- **Sound and music:** weight and synchronization, intelligibility, musical preparation/payoff and which element owns attention. Direct listening and signal checks are separate evidence.
- **Production/reuse/rights:** viable representation, dependencies, revision needs, accepted-source limits, resource and rights boundaries.

These are review perspectives, not claimed human specialist qualifications or independent model judgments. A single agent can miss things across all of them. Where knowledge is weak, identify it, research a relevant primary reference or run a discriminating control; do not fill the gap with confident prose.

## Brief fields — complete before substantial execution

Keep the filled brief in the existing sequence work package, linking this template. Bind its revision in the task packet alongside actual source inputs.

1. **Identity and authority:** sequence/shot/beat IDs and timing; accepted creative/source versions; what is fixed, flexible or undecided. Keep Shot Intent separate from the changeable realization plan.
2. **Audience result:** what viewers should understand, feel and notice; extraordinary moment and contrast; what must remain recognizable. State hypotheses, not invented audience research.
3. **Visibility and criticality:** actual framing, screen size, exposure and action; required quality-floor behaviors, dramatic priorities and optional refinements. An out-of-frame assumption needs evidence and a reopening trigger. Low salience cannot excuse a visible structural failure.
4. **Applicable lenses and predicted failures:** actively scan the lenses above. Explain omissions. Include likely unreported distractions and adjacent interfaces, not only the current Director defect list.
5. **Strategy per important behavior:** intended motion/deformation/response; chosen representation and why it fits; owner and coordinate space of contacts; inputs/supports, coupled dependencies and known limitations. Choose authored animation, constrained procedural motion, simulation or a declared hybrid on evidence, not prestige. Specify which matters must be physically consistent and which may be intentionally heightened without contradiction.
6. **Earliest representative proof:** cheap actual-framing motion plus revealing controls before polish/full rendering. Define observable pass/fail, a negative control, required references and what outcome changes the method. Numbers support visual/audio judgment, not replace it.
7. **Review, revision and exit:** evidence per requirement; changed-input invalidation; uncertainty; remaining optional work and reopening triggers; release dependency unlocked. Use the integrated internal gate, not a new handoff at every lens.

Use this row format for the important behaviors; a generic list of body parts is insufficient:

| Beat / audience-visible requirement | Lens; predicted distraction and importance | Method, owner and interfaces | Early proof / explicit failure condition | Evidence, status and reopening trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ID/time/framing; intended read] | [Why viewers might notice; mandatory/optional] | [Chosen route and rationale, not merely “make realistic”] | [Actual action/control; what would falsify it] | [Reference + artifact/frame/time/hash; PASS/FAIL/NOT_RUN; changed use] |

## Enforcement and learning

Before dispatch, the agent must check that every planned beat is covered or explicitly inherits an applicable row; that critical behaviors have a method and early proof; and that the knowledge packet includes related anatomy/contact/CFX/geography obligations. An unknown entity/domain requires source and relationship reconciliation. Before internal admission, run a **cold audience read** and then a **failure-seeking technical read**; both belong to the same integrated artifact. Separately label real-time playback, chronological frame inspection, isolated views and direct listening. Missing evidence stays NOT_RUN.

Reopen affected rows when camera, action, timing, costume, contact, scale or delivery framing changes. Re-rank residual distractions once larger problems are fixed; this is how an earlier tolerated fist can become the remaining blocker. Do not spend effort on hypothetical future uses, but do not call missing anatomy a hidden polish item.

For each Director-reported defect, record: anticipated or missed; requirement absent, retrieval missed, method failed, evidence missing, or internal judgment wrongly passed; repair; adjacent consequences; reusable lesson and a future test. Measure escaped defects, avoidable revision rounds and cost-to-accepted footage—not just number of checkboxes. If the right rule was retrieved but a bad render was passed, fix evaluation rather than merely adding more memory.

**Implemented now:** this operating contract, AGENTS requirement, motion-template link, mandatory retrieval action and a routing regression test. **Not implemented:** automatic extraction of every shot, automatic verification that a brief is substantively complete, independent expert review or a universal perceptual judge. Pilot gates validate structured declarations/hashes, not artistic truth. Full schema validation/launcher coverage belongs to later bounded Factory work; manual enforcement remains required now. No additional agents, spend, installations or new character production are authorized here.
