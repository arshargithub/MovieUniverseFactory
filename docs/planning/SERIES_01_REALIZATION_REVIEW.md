This is one production question with staged deliverables, not three new generic campaigns. ## Strategic recommendation — first-class, optional generation

Factory should own canon, approved references, scene/shot intent, pacing, asset versions and acceptance evidence. A generative provider is a replaceable realization worker, not the source of truth for recurring identity or geography. Persistent representation need not always mean 3D; it can include approved multi-view references and qualified identity conditioning, with 3D preferred where demonstrated control/reuse justifies it.

| Requirement | Recommended candidate route, not yet a blanket qualification |
|---|---|
| Recurring leads, interactions, exact action/camera and repeatable revision | Persistent assets and structured staging; direct render or verified hybrid finish |
| Standalone atmospheric/establishing shots with flexible continuity | Generative video can compete for final pixels; still subject to look, rights and editorial acceptance |
| Stylization, environment extension or localized appearance changes | Reference/video-guided generation, verifying intended changes and preservation of everything important |
| Early motion/mood exploration | Cheap bounded generation where it informs a decision; never confuse exploration with canon or a usable take |

No mandate that every shot pass through an AI finish, no fixed percentage of generative footage, and no declaration that Runway or all generated character shots are permanently unsuitable. Evaluate by accepted seconds, revision preservation, reusable work, retries, Director review/engineering time and total cost-to-acceptance—not nominal dollars per generated second. Keep unknown engineering tokens unknown.

Current provider evidence: Runway's [image-to-video guide](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/48324313115155-Image-to-Video-Prompting-Guide) describes the image as initial visual guidance and recommends reducing ambiguity. Its [Aleph 2.0 guide](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/52150503729171-Aleph-2-0-Prompting-Guide) documents video editing guided by a changed frame, including restyling. These documented interfaces support investigating a different control strategy; provider examples/claims are not evidence that our shot or identity will be preserved.
