## Reference and diagnosis

Reviewed approved `frontal-v02-individualized.png` for the intended soft, unadorned neck/collarbone character. Anatomical orientation checked against [OpenStax: axial muscles of the head, neck and back](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-3-axial-muscles-of-the-head-neck-and-back) and [pectoral girdle](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-1-the-pectoral-girdle): paired oblique SCM paths toward sternum/clavicle, shallow central suprasternal depression, and lateral collarbone arcs rather than a vertical central ridge. These inform an artistic approximation, not medical/anatomy qualification. No reference artwork is imported into the asset.

The previous layered corrections (section interpolation, radial repair, relief and later vertical compression) produced a circumferential neck band and a pinched central collarbone region. Candidate 01 replaces the full lower-neck range with a single quintic radial bridge. It fails artistically: sampled under-chin slopes propagate into a deeply concave, vertically streaked throat. Native controls pass but this is not selected.

Candidate 02 uses a smooth oval target with compact boundary blending, avoiding the propagation of sampled slope noise. Its throat is smoother but the rear oval depth creates another upper-neck bulge and the shoulder flare begins too high; anatomical definition is too subdued. Not selected. Candidate 03 distinguishes front/rear oval depth, delays lateral shoulder flare and restores gentle paired neck/collarbone relief. Final inspection and disposition follow below; technical passes are not Director acceptance.
