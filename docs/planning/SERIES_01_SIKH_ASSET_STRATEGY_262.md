## Face recommendation

Start with a configurable **whole adult male base**, using MakeHuman/MPFB morph targets rather than more local offsets on the failed bust. A morph target is a predefined shape adjustment; the system offers overall proportions and individual facial-feature adjustments. Its documentation also warns that changes require refitting the rig and attached assets. [Modeling documentation](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/characters/modeling.html).

Protect the accepted v03's rugged elongated impression, strong nose/brow, substantial broad beard and humane expression. Keep its painted visual language. Exact cheek contours, hidden jaw geometry and brush marks are not reconstruction requirements. Facial hair reduces exposed skin work but raises the importance of beard silhouette and attachment. Eyes, brows, nose, visible cheeks/temples and the transition into the beard still carry identity and acting. Do not select anatomy from a generic ethnic stereotype or conceal an unsuitable head with hair.

Existing local MPFB inventory contains a base mesh, standard rig/weights and one female macro-target file; it is **not** a verified complete male generator. The Blender Studio bundle is also already local. Inspect target/base version compatibility before reuse. Prefer data-only acquisition and a reviewed fixed handler where economical; a new extension installation retains its separate approval boundary. Do not write a replacement character generator merely to avoid discussing an installation.

The official [asset-pack catalog](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html) lists CC0 system eyes/teeth, male skins, facial detail targets and beard assets. Core asset permission does not license all community uploads indiscriminately. Check the selected package and embedded credits on acquisition.

## Beard and material shortlist

[Bodyparts05](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/bodyparts05.html) is a listed 6 MB CC0 beard/moustache pack. Specific candidates include RehmanPolanski's beard_viking and moustache_viking, WDG's scruffy_beard and grinsegold's beard_sigmund_wip. These are **listed mesh donors**, not groom-qualified assets; MPFB loads them as clothing assets. Inspect their silhouette, roots, transparency, texture dependence and editable structure before choosing one. Names such as Viking are catalog labels, not the intended character design.

Use a donor for coherent broad volume and natural breakup, then adapt the cheek transition and rounded irregular bottom. Reject narrow pointed/goatee or rigid curtain results. An inexpensive card-based beard may work at the intended framing, but it must survive the uncovering hand, bow and launch without turning flat or exposing black planes. Native strand grooming is the alternative if that discriminating test fails, not an automatic prerequisite.

Male skins and eyebrow packs are candidate inputs for warm, rugged, illustrated surfaces. Avoid baked beard/shadow texture beneath a second geometric beard. No selected pack has yet been imported or qualified for this Sikh.

## Motorcycle candidate

[Royal Enfield by rohitart3d](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/royal-enfield-2d17cbb6c8be4d4ca0d0ba5f115ff364) is currently listed as downloadable under CC Attribution, linked to CC-BY4.0. The live listing reports535.8k triangles and274.9k vertices. Its viewer shows a useful classic single-cylinder silhouette: round lamp, spoked wheels, rounded tank and separate-looking saddle. **Viewer inspection is not confirmation of separable native parts, textures or a rig.** It has not been downloaded this pass.

This is a plausible component donor, not an exact1940s military bike. Proposed adaptation: low broad drag bar, restrained worn military finish, selective removal of modern fittings/branding and screen-relevant proportion changes. Inspect steering, wheel separation, suspension, tire contact and rider fit first. Reject it if fused detail makes those changes more expensive than another donor or a hybrid build. Attribution must retain creator, source, license and modification notice. No claim of trademark clearance follows from mesh licensing.

The opening camera puts the bike close to the viewer, so it is a higher-value acquisition target than background decoration. A static detailed model still needs a coherent mechanical rig and a supported stop; no need to construct working engine internals.
