## Applied and carry-forward learnings

1. **Allocate music across the trailer.** Before locking one character cue, map the other entrances and ensemble ending against source peaks. A locally strong cue may spend the best material too early. Preserve reversible source-to-edit landmarks.
2. **Isolate comparisons.** Swap only the music component of a fixed common mix, preserve picture packets and avoid independent master normalization that also changes speech/effects. Verify rather than merely intend equivalence.
3. **Voice source and mix are separate problems.** Quiet final words need phrase/source assessment; global music ducking alone cannot guarantee clarity and may kill momentum. Key-word RMS is diagnostic only. Listening must decide whether “races” and “impeccable” actually read.
4. **Review the encoded deliverable.** An internally detected SRT inference bug made captions huge/misplaced despite reasonable numeric settings. Explicit portrait ASS design space fixed it; rejected control is preserved. Render success and source typography are insufficient.
5. **Environment failure is not aesthetic failure.** Sandbox Metal initialization failed before scene load; authorized GPU access succeeded. The earlier binary-archive workaround and persistent-OFF policy remain scoped and evidenced, not proven universal root-cause claims.
6. **No extra render for an editorial comparison.**246 reused the completed shared245picture, avoiding duplicate native work. Final rendering is expensive; keep audio/graphic/source choices reversible in post.
