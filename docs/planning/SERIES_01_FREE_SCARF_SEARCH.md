## License nuance

CGTrader's [official terms](https://www.cgtrader.com/pages/terms-and-conditions), section 21B.1, attach the no-AI restriction to product use for machine learning/training of neural-network models, including generative AI. The label does not itself say “Blender automation prohibited.” Keep the veil collection in the shortlist, but do not claim this settles agent-mediated inspection or every future Factory use. Before adoption, clarify any ambiguity for the intended workflow; do not upload the asset or its renders to generative services pending clearance. No license accepted, creator contacted or definitive legal clearance claimed here.

Meshy's [headscarf gallery](https://www.meshy.ai/tags/headscarf) claims CC0 for premade gallery items and account-based downloads; this is not proof of the terms or topology of every item. Tripo example terms remain UNKNOWN rather than importing Meshy's policy into a different service.
