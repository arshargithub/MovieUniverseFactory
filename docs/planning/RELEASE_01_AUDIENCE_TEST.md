# Release 01 — trailer/intro audience test

Established exchange 53. Strategy accepted; creative treatment and execution details open. At exchange 54 the Director supplies entrance/archival/assembly ideas; treatment development is now active in [trailer concept](SERIES_01_TRAILER_CONCEPT.md).

## Purpose and scope

The trailer/intro is the first audience test for the series, not an additional standalone proof film. Assess whether a reimagined history-inspired ensemble, presented in stylized cinematic 3D with a graphic-novel sensibility, attracts attention and creates interest in another installment. Restrain technology and extraordinary abilities for this release; exact allowances remain to be selected. Preserve culturally specific, multidimensional characters and an intelligible audience promise. Reach and returning viewership are explicit project goals, not guarantees.

Primary audience direction: North American/European and other non-South-Asian viewers, especially relevant action/adventure/animation audiences; include culturally connected viewers in evaluation rather than assuming their approval or treating either group as uniform. No recruitment/outreach authorized by this brief.

## What the test can and cannot establish

- **Attraction:** proposed measures include platform-appropriate exposure-to-watch behavior, qualified views and early retention. Raw views alone do not demonstrate attachment; paid/organic distribution and exposure must be distinguished if used.
- **Engagement/comprehension:** retention/completion alongside understandable stakes, recognizable character differences and emotional interest. Do not require prior historical knowledge or infer comprehension solely from a completed play.
- **Intent to return:** proposed signals include follows/subscriptions, interest in a next release and voluntary requests to see more. These are proxies, not proof of recurring viewership.
- **Actual return:** measure across subsequent releases using available platform analytics; a single trailer cannot qualify durable audience retention. No assumed access to cross-platform personal tracking.
- **Representation:** seek specific feedback on character dimensionality, cultural/religious detail and perceived stereotypes from relevant informed viewers/reviewers. This is distinct from popularity and cannot be reduced to one community-wide approval score.

Before publication, C1/C2/C4 must define platform, audience, duration/format, metric definitions, observation window, exposure context, practical thresholds and what results would prompt revision versus further production. No invented universal benchmark, guaranteed virality or automatic causal conclusion from a small test. Insufficient exposure is inconclusive, not proof the concept failed.

## Lean execution and dependencies

1. Receive Director's trailer ideas; establish intended audience promise and what is deliberately withheld.
2. Define only the world/technology/ability rules needed for honest representation and coherent scenes; create reference-driven look comparisons using the same trailer material.
3. Approve an inexpensive animatic before costly shot production; reuse trailer shots for applicable E1–E4 checks and measure cost/time. No redundant audience-test movie or generic campaign per tool/style.
4. Review craft/readability and cultural details before public test. Private quality review does not become an unrequested separate release.
5. Publish only after explicit authorization and required rights/credits/output checks. Observe, document uncertainty, and use findings to adjust episode production; do not assume all standalone Factory features must be finished to learn from the trailer. Required durability/safe-operation controls still apply.
