### Internal review procedure carried forward

1. Before building, use the existing shot/beat brief to state audience read, signature impact and the most likely visible failure. Select the applicable expertise lenses and adjacent interfaces, not a studio org chart.
2. Convert every critical retrieved lesson into a named action and falsifiable early proof in the work package. Retrieve/reconcile the new lead's actual decisions; copying Pashtun-specific identity or assumptions is not reuse.
3. Before claiming success, inspect both the known-bad example and the relevant accepted target. State what physically distinguishes them. Test the complete required region and partial/full motion, actual camera and screen scale. Do not substitute a test count or a prettier predecessor.
4. Separate static observation, chronological frame inspection, real-time motion and listening. For temporal/audio claims, require the actual appropriate evidence/review route. Existing253 acceptance supplies the human audiovisual decision for this exact unit, not every new shot. Planned integrated Director reviews remain necessary for creative acceptance; they must not become technical defect discovery by default.
5. Preserve acceptance scope: an approved deliberate difference is not an invitation to polish. Changed camera/action can reopen a contact/cloth/visibility test. If evidence is missing, retain NOT_RUN and complete the relevant internal check; escalate only a real capability/authority decision.
6. Record issue/review IDs, first-detection phase, whether anticipated, source of discovery, repeat ID, root-cause category, revision radius, artifact version and disposition. Do not reset a recurring failure merely by renaming the pass. Compare the next sequence at the same review stage and quality floor; no promised percentage efficiency gain.

This is now an operational calibration set plus current-state repair, not a new knowledge platform. It cannot guarantee the agent will never misjudge an image. Next real work is a prospective shadow test: compare the packet with relevant deep sources and use early integrated proof before expensive production. Omitted coverage or a reviewer escape becomes a new regression case and a process correction.
