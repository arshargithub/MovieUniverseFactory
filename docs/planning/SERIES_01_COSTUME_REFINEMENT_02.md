## Learnings

Use clay and object-ID checks early: a texture seam, a shape discontinuity, a skin/cloth intersection and the wrong underlying garment require different fixes. High-resolution close-ups exposed errors hidden in wider views. Fit the **evaluated** cloth and the ribbon's **width**, not only centerlines/control points. A nominal front-to-back strap is not evidence that the shoulder loop reads correctly. Check its actual sleeve contact and visible route from the side. Protect the face independently of the neck; source mesh identity alone does not prove the visible derivative is unchanged. Do not call a visually rejected native-success candidate a pass.
