## Outcome progress and resource allocation

Define preweighted, nonoverlapping release milestones with supported conditions.
For milestone j, weight w_j represents the agreed production scope and q_j is a
supported completion state (binary at its gate). Then E=sum(w_j*q_j). Decompose
weights without increasing their sum; reweight only by versioned reconciliation,
never to manufacture improvement. Keep finished usable media separate from partial
asset/proof progress and from knowledge gained by R&D. Revoked proofs revoke their
credit. Reusing an asset does not automatically qualify its new shot.

For any W-cycle window:

```
retained production yield = (E_after - E_before)/W
process/admin share = admin_cycles/all_cycles
milestone burn = cycles_used_on_milestone/milestone_cycle_envelope
```

Eneed not increase every cycle. A discriminating failure can create valuable R&D
knowledge, but cannot be counted as finished footage or reset production stagnation.
Per-critical-feature subproofs provide a finer signal than complete sequence gates;
they must be predefined, meaningful, actually qualified and retained at integration.

Set a finite remaining envelope R, allocate it among milestone work, engineering/
R&D, process work and contingency, with total reservations <=R. On the Director's
equal-effort assumption, an uncompleted full sequence has baseline weight1/12;
allocation is prospective remaining work, not automatic sunk-cost reimbursement.
Each micro-task/method draws from its enclosing milestone. No new budgets arise
from splitting, renaming, model switches or scope labels.

Illustration only: if a sequence has200cycles remaining and20%is deliberately
reserved for uncertain methods, that pool is40cycles; at100cycles it is20. The
fraction is a policy/allocation choice to approve and calibrate, not a universal
20%rule. This is more defensible than a six-cycle cap independent of sequence
value, but it still needs an upper ceiling and evidence-driven early exit.

Use estimates/ranges only where supported. If a credible completion estimate
exceeds the remaining envelope, flag the plan for reallocation/representation
change. If no estimate is supportable, classify qualification as uncertain R&D
with a finite allowance; do not invent a probability or completion date.

## Concrete forward-momentum watchdog proposed

Apply this to the stable production-blocking capability and its milestone, not
only each newly named method. Adoption/calibration remain pending.

| Trigger | Deterministic consequence |
|---|---|
|Every cycle completes/fails|Update consumed reservations, proof/feature state, invalidations and next eligible release dependency.|
|Second ineffective intervention on the same feature|Internal scope/method/constraint challenge; not an automatic Director handoff or retirement.|
|Ten charged cycles since last retained meaningful critical subproof|Mandatory internal production-investment review: remaining scope, current proof trend, spent/remaining allocation and actual alternative path.|
|Twenty charged cycles without retained meaningful critical subproof|HOLD further construction on that capability across methods/models/micro-tasks. Agent must issue an explicit stop/reallocation recommendation.|
|Allocated method/milestone/resource envelope exhausted|HOLD regardless of recent partial progress; no implied new reservation.|
|Immediate material falsifier, contradictory proof, missing authority, rights or required evidence|Existing fail-closed rules prevail before another launch.|

The10/20settings are provisional operational watchdog candidates, not optimal
thresholds or a guarantee that20cycles can solve anatomy. They separate review
cadence from a practical stop. Earlier clear failure can stop immediately. A real
qualified proof on the last allowed attempt can pass its scoped review; it does
not grant more effort or qualify the final moving asset automatically.

Let s be charged cycles since the latest still-valid meaningful subproof. Also
maintain s_f for each active blocking feature f: cycles with declared/observed
impact on f since its latest retained meaningful subproof. If any s_f reaches20,
dependent construction holds even if unrelated features recently improved.
Affected-feature coverage is bound in the plan and checked against actual effects;
unknown/unreconciled impact cannot silently omit a blocker. A new
method, proposed remedy, numerical error reduction, repeated retro or additional
permission does not reset s. A genuine qualified subproof may reset it; trivial
new checklist items may not. Keep the unresolved feature history alongside s so
easy adjacent work cannot hide one permanent bottleneck. A critical regression
reopens affected credit and its unresolved age. Child work shares the original
parent allocation even after a legitimate subproof.

Ordinary “ten more cycles” can enlarge permission but does not override an adopted
production stop. Further exploration would need an explicit finite R&D exception
acknowledging the production disposition, new discriminating question and production
tradeoff. The agent should recommend against it when the evidence does not warrant it.
