## Director's requested outcome

A24–30second sequence using the existing horse/rider where feasible:

1. Start higher than the accepted aerial camera, centered behind horse/rider without lateral offset. Descend and sweep to the right, hold a parallel full-body side view, then settle into an offset frontal tracking view.
2. A more aggressive racing stance and natural coordinated rider body motion.
3. Investigate racing-speed gallop and fuller stride. The accepted scene travels12m/s;16–17m/s is a proposed research target, not an admitted capability. Do not merely accelerate the same video or force stretched limbs.
4. A larger clearly visible trailing dust cloud, with ground contact readable independently.
5. Higher-fidelity background: actual nearby grass geometry, improved terrain/material variation and suitable vegetation rather than flat green surfaces. Choose coherent scenery within the later approved asset policy.
6. Dramatic dusty deceleration to a halt, followed by a balanced rear and return to standing. Horse and rider must respond together; instant root-motion stopping is insufficient.
7. A natural rider dismount after the horse settles: release relevant contacts, leg clearance, weight transfer, hand support and stable ground landing.
