## Sequence contract

Approximately 13 seconds in native 9:16: speed → obstacle → assessment → committed alternate route. The woman is pursuing her purpose, not automatically fleeing an enemy. Horse/rider feel powerful and coordinated, never literally flying. No visible blood, graphic injury, horse harm, impossible recovery or supernatural leap. Existing trailer runtime remains 60–75 seconds; final placement/compression must preserve the other two leads and thematic/mission beats.

Use four shots, six planning panels. Screen direction below is a provisional continuity convention, not a new canon decision. No new stills generated for this draft.

## What to keep fixed across panels

- Same approved face, adult maturity, fullness, eye/hair colors and simple indigo clothing; no decorative makeup/jewelry. Portrait pair is identity guidance, not an upright posture lock.
- Same horse, tack, cloth arrangement, travel direction, light and terrain landmarks. Horse/tack design is not yet approved; carry one provisional design consistently rather than silently freeze it.
- Rider hands/reins, seat/support, head/torso and horse gait must be planned together. Do not treat a racing pose as a rigid body glued to a running horse. No detailed gait mechanics are claimed from unreviewed references.
- Cloth/dust may amplify motion, not hide anatomy failures. Keep face/route readable in vertical framing and leave caption/UI-safe composition margins for later delivery checks.
