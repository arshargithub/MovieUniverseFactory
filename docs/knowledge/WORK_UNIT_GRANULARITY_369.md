## Motion

A motion work unit is the smallest subset of a beat containing meaningful motion
work that can be visually reviewed and judged independently. Choose its boundary
by the audience-visible outcome and causal action, not seconds, bones, parameters
or the convenience of the implementation. Simple action may span many seconds;
dense rearing preparation may occupy only a few seconds.

Before execution answer: what can a viewer judge here, what preceding cause and
following response must remain visible, and what pose/velocity/contact state must
the next unit inherit? Include all coupled body, rider and prop contributions
necessary for that judgment. A hoof-only crop cannot prove a powerful brace; a
whole rear need not be rebuilt to judge its preparation. Expand an under-sized
unit if the result cannot be judged without guessing; split an oversized unit if
independent failure causes compete. Retain boundary footage and an eventual
whole-action integration check. Units are internal work boundaries, not new cuts
or automatic Director handoffs. No fixed duration, timebox or allowance reset.

Current application: preparation includes the single forehoof brace, hindquarter
loading and head/neck/body release through first lift. Director369 identifies
insufficient squat: compare stifle/hock flexion, pelvis/body gathering and planted
support against the reference. Lowering the root alone or changing playback speed
does not establish a loaded, powerful animal. These are visible animation cues;
do not claim recovered or simulated forces from video.

## Appearance equivalent

Use the smallest coherent, visually judgeable form/construction/interface outcome,
with enough surrounding anatomy, garment layers, poses, views and lighting to
judge its integration and membership in the same film. Examples: an ear/temple/
hairline transition, or shoulder/shawl/harness layering. A texture patch or one
mesh is an implementation unit and may be too small; a whole costumed principal
may be too large. Prove whole forms and interfaces before fibers or surface noise.

Specify intended framing, accepted comparator, revealing reverse/partial poses,
allowed changes and frozen interfaces. Judge the complete region in those views,
then reintegrate. Static appearance cannot qualify motion. Moving forms need
motion-conditioned checks even when the task began as appearance work.

At369 this was a manual rule. **374 subsequently implemented** a distinct
appearance discipline with visual-reference roles, pose/view coverage and
interfaces (see `results/sikh374/LEARNINGS.md`). **386 now adds** the required
scope/causal challenge and evaluated-intervention contract in
[the current guide](MICRO_APPEARANCE_WORKFLOW_386.md). Coverage enforcement is
implemented; semantic unit sizing and artistic judgment remain agent duties.
Do not mislabel appearance as faithful_motion or infer art from software tests.
The original369 learning capture alone granted no construction authority.

## Carry-forward and evidence

The mandatory367 guide links this supplement; future unit planning reads both.
Record the outcome, causal context, coupled interfaces and why the chosen extent
is independently judgeable in the strategy/challenge. Review the entire unit,
not just the edited property, and preserve accepted features across integration.
Retrospectives distinguish wrong unit size from wrong method or permissive
judgment. Track escaped defects, repeated repairs and useful Director decisions;
one encouraging result is not a measured general efficiency gain.
