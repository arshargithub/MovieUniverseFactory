# Expert-lens planning: derivation, proof and original-intent review

**378 extension:** [reference readiness](REFERENCE_READINESS_378.md) requires
expert derivation to map requirements to purpose-qualified reference needs,
observed features and explicit authority exclusions. Test adequacy before
choosing a method; a physical reference does not silently govern film style.

Adopted and implemented locally at Director exchange328. Goal: extract more
value from creative direction and reduce avoidable technical corrections, not
reduce useful Director choices or lower the quality floor.

## What changed

The previous shot brief contained good manual guidance, but the staged controller
did not require its application. A valid brief could contain two generic checks
and a strategy paragraph. It now rejects that production brief.

For each trusted beat intent the planner must assess story/performance,
camera/editorial, animation/physics, anatomy/contact, effects/environment,
sound/music and production/reuse. These are perspectives, not seven workers.
Additional specialties belong in the relevant derivations. Every applied lens
must produce concrete linked requirements; non-application needs a reason.

Each requirement records its derivation, method, coupled interfaces, early proof
at intended framing, observable failure, reopening trigger, priority, origin
(Director/reference/craft inference) and reference IDs. Reference roles and
fidelity contracts remain mandatory in all stages. Exact approved identities
and faithful motion references are not interchangeable with directional images.

Before admission the planning stage challenges intent coverage, interdisciplinary
conflicts, omitted failures and scope tradeoffs. The trusted parent reads this
challenge and seals its hash. This is **not an independent fourth expert** or a
claim that hashing verifies reasoning. Unresolved issues block admission. Routine
in-scope methods do not need a Director approval; material creative/authority
conflicts do. Lightweight retrieval/display/link tasks retain their separate route.

Execution records how every requirement was applied against the same plan digest.
This is traceability, not visual proof. Review receives original intent and
references, not just a checklist or the builder's success narrative. It must scan
for omitted requirements and report per-beat intent observations. An intent FAIL
vetoes otherwise passing checks; a planning gap routes to replanning, not another
blind implementation patch. In-scope replan repairs consume the existing correction
allowance rather than reset it or create an unnecessary Director handoff.

Intent observations cover supported evidence only. Direct motion/audio remain
Director-owned under313; missing playback cannot excuse a frame-visible defect.
No current scene or existing rejection was newly passed by this implementation.

## Retrospective test: what would have changed?

[Four reconstructed cases](../../results/workflow328/cases.json) use source-indexed
earlier direction and separate later feedback. [Evaluation outputs](../../results/workflow328/evaluation/report.json)
preserve input excerpts, source hashes and plans. Three cases use chronological
message offsets; the fourth uses320's existing strategy against321's later audit.
The author already knew the history. This is a **known-history counterfactual
audit, not blind prediction, a fresh model trial or demonstrated better footage**.

| Case | Derived intervention | Honest conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Danger and escape route | Two-target visibility, explicit viewer/actor coordinates, gaze/route causality, sufficient decision distance | Earlier planning could have prevented or exposed several avoidable camera/geography corrections. It cannot predict the exact later preferred camera. |
| Racing rider | Common stride/support driver with different pelvis, torso, neck and head response; root-bound secondary motion; close-framing proof | Stiff neck, mane and tail were foreseeable from racing intent, before those defects were named. Correct implementation and perceptual rejection still require evidence. |
| Voice and chosen music | Phrase-level attention hierarchy, key-word intelligibility, music continuity, integrated opening proof | Could expose masking and over-ducking earlier. Removing quoted narration and selecting a particular ramp remain legitimate creative choices. |
| Nihang and crowd | Full reference pose family, one leader/near warrior proof before replication, socially motivated attention and movement | Many obligations were already in320. This was principally a method/evidence/judgment failure; another checklist alone would not have prevented it. |

Possible enhancements beyond defect repair: a more legible intelligent route
choice; a composed rider who nevertheless feels force and speed; a musical phrase
that remains alive under speech; a leader/crowd response that reads as a social
exchange rather than moving obstacles. These are execution hypotheses, not
unrequested changes to approved creative direction.

All four reconstructed plans pass the new structural expert-plan contract.
All12 malformed variants (missing lens, missing method, stale challenge) fail.
Crucially, all4 deliberately bad but well-formed methods pass that same schema.
The software does not understand artistic truth. This negative control must stay
visible; do not present structural tests as proof of expert judgment or fewer
future Director inputs. No percentage improvement is established.

## Application and next proof

`production_workflow.Controller.packet` now carries the expert contract and
instructions into actual planner/reviewer stage packets; `SerialRunner` forwards
them without a supplement override. Controller submissions enforce coverage,
authority, freshness and original-intent vetoes. `expert_planning.seal_planner_response`
hashes a complete response but never fills missing reasoning with defaults.
Synthetic test fixtures are explicitly not production-plan generators.

Historical323/324 briefs are not silently upgraded or grandfathered into
production. The324 live adapter now stops before inference pending explicit
migration; its old results remain historical evidence. No new call allowance was
created. A complete production-authoring bridge remains unfinished; this is not
an unavoidable end-to-end autonomous studio harness.

For the next isolated Nihang/rearing/crowd package, freeze the derived plan and
predicted failures before construction. Prove coupled horse/rider poses and one
leader/near warrior at actual framing before replication. On subsequent Director
review classify every observation as creative choice, missing requirement,
retrieval gap, method failure, evidence omission or judgment escape. Compare
predicted-versus-escaped defects, repeated regressions, avoidable handoffs and
active work/render time. Do not count expected Director AV review as waste.
This prospective test, not a retrospective percentage, will establish improvement.

This source is mandatory via the core instructions and active plan. It is not
silently added to the15-source/49-record retrieval pilot; fresh packets still
require manual source/relationship reconciliation. Current pass used a fresh
packet before implementation. Future catalog expansion needs reviewed records
and calibration, not simply rehashing source changes.
