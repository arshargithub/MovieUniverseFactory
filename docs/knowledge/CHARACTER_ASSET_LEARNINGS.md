Working explanation: the image specifies appearance, not the hidden garment construction. A mesh is connected sample points/faces; topology is their connectivity; a rest state defines unstressed dimensions and sometimes preferred curvature. Rigging supplies controls, skin weights map controls to surface motion, and pose correctives repair deformation in particular poses. Simulation supplies time-dependent response given construction, supports, forces and contacts; it does not choose an attractive costume. Pinning keeps selected cloth near prescribed positions, trading freedom for control. Collision proxies are simplified stand-ins, whose shape and motion must match what is rendered. Thickness used for contact is distinct from visible thickness. UV/material data establish surface appearance; large silhouette folds cannot be repaired by painted weave. Cached trajectories preserve a solve, not its artistic validity. Rig-following, authored secondary motion and physically simulated motion are different valid tools that need honest labels.
