## Reuse selection and qualification envelope

Evaluate existing assets, rigs, motions, materials and workflows before custom
construction. Compare total cost-to-acceptance: acquisition, adaptation, contact
repair, same-film matching, revalidation, revisions and rights. Free acquisition
does not mean inexpensive integration; reuse must not be forced. Keep a rejected
or merely investigated option with its reason; never promote a listing to tested
compatibility. A material cannot solve motion and motion cannot solve anatomy.

Extend the existing provenance/manifests with sidecar resource records: immutable
source/version hashes; original provenance and rights record; reuse mode;
dependency/interface requirements; tested conditions; evidence, known failures,
and measured adaptation effort (unknown stays null). Historical manifests remain
unchanged. The resource validator checks scoped declarations and artifact freshness,
not license interpretation, visual truth, or automatic admission of all legacy work.
The caller must enforce its result. No new database or parallel source of truth.

Qualification is always scoped. Exact scene/rig/runtime/framing reuse differs
from adapting to another actor or shot. Missing rights, source or evidence, a
changed dependency, untested conditions or open blocking defects prevent a
qualified-use claim. An assessed/in-progress record remains useful without
pretending it passed. Rejected resources retain their negative evidence.

## Vehicle support and secondary response

D316-01: directly fitting chassis pitch/height to every rotating tire minimum
can transmit road/tread chatter to the whole rider and motorcycle. Preserve
terrain detail, wheel rolling/slip and actual contacts while separating supported
wheel motion from chassis response. Check fork/axle, lamp/housing, hands/grips,
boots/pegs and planted feet after any filter. Smoother chassis alone is not a pass.
317's filtered response is a shot-specific hypothesis, not a physics simulator.
Reopen on changed road frequency, speed, wheelbase, suspension travel or framing.

## Coordinated animal performance and coat

D316-02–04: more animated joints or improved silhouettes do not establish natural
performance. Inspect reference phases in actual elapsed time, joint axes and hoof
orientation, support/weight transfer, brief active apex, neck/head response,
rider balance, reins/spear and landing effects together. A large wrist rotation
is a diagnostic finding, not proof of the entire cause. Preserve pending direct
motion judgment under313; timed frames are not continuous playback.

317's saved-native probe found an IK socket still driving the visible hoof
downstream of the FK leg. Trace ownership through the rendered endpoint, not
only the named wrist/toe controls. Grounded versus airborne ownership changes
require both seam and support checks throughout the transfer. Quaternion bones
and render-time animation evaluation also invalidated two earlier diagnostic
edits: a control must demonstrably affect the evaluated rendered pose.

Coat must read as an animal at intended framing: anatomical regions, muzzle/eyes,
pigmentation, restrained highlights, directional short nap and groom response.
Macro noise and sheen alone can read as clay/metal. Test material versus lighting
with matched controls. Any added nap must share actual deformation/root ownership;
static fuzz drifting away is a new failure, not an improvement.

## Crowd and adjacent terrain

D316-05–06: people yield with anticipation, weight shift, individually timed
non-crossing steps, planted support, torso/gaze intent and connected costume/props.
Random perpetual sway or synchronized sideways translation is not life. Rebake
visible trousers and sleeves when hidden joints change; verify near and far actors
and corridor clearance in actual shots. A rigged motion library still requires
compatible skeleton, proportions, support and prop handling.

317's crowd375 frame exposed separated trousers and boots despite passing
boot-to-ground checks. Viewport-excluded source limbs had not evaluated during
the garment bake; animation curves alone were not evidence of motion. Keep bake
sources evaluated but render-hidden, bind the actual sequence range (the old
helper ended at528 while this sequence has696 frames), and measure evaluated
garment endpoints against visible boots/hands through the required timeline.
Candidate06 corrected the seam but exposed0.85m split-like strides. Reachability
and contact are necessary, not sufficient: inspect clothed stance, step width,
body support and temporal naturalness separately. Shorter steps remain a motion
hypothesis until the complete performance is judged. See
`results/sikh317/CARRY_FORWARD.md` and preserved rejected candidate evidence.

Enrich a ditch by drainage and use: rooted clusters, broken stubble, deposits,
erosion and irregular transitions. Keep paths and occupied crossings usable;
do not replace a bare strip with a uniformly scattered or continuous green strip.
Support-changing geometry invalidates foot/wheel/FX evidence.
