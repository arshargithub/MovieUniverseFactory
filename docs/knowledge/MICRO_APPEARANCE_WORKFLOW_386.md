## Admission

New covered local-native work units use `version:2`. Legacy immutable/offline
version1 remains readable; it does not qualify a new native admission. Existing
trusted `unit_required` references and the core instructions identify covered
work. The system cannot infer a missing artistic unit from dishonest operation
classification or constrain arbitrary OS tools.

Keep the established positive criteria, reference purposes, original task,
allowed/frozen domains, boundary state, views/poses and rejected controls. Add:

- `scope_decision`: keep/split/merge, rationale, independent judgeability,
  necessary coupled context, worst-first and integrated proofs, one current
  outcome among considered current/coupled/deferred outcomes, original parent
  task and bound shared allowance. A split is a work boundary, not a film cut.
- `causal_experiment`: unknown, hypothesis, semantic method family, failed
  alternatives, discriminator, positive/failing predictions, failure response
  and actual bound evidence. Another coefficient/name/hash is not by itself a
  new causal experiment.
- `scope_challenge`: explicit counterexample, source evidence and judgment that
  the proposed extent is meaningful. The separate CHALLENGING stage additionally
  binds the exact unit digest and compares independent outcome, coupled context,
  positive target, causal test and interfaces against actual source bindings.
- `dependencies`: exact prior proof/source bindings, affected interfaces,
  preserve/revalidate disposition and reason. Registered proof reuse retains
  the existing resource-manifest/retrospective checks too.

The workflow checks coverage, hashes, declared scope and counts. It cannot prove
that a unit is actually small enough, the anatomy is correct or the challenge
truthful. Preserve the semantically wrong but well-formed negative control.
Do not substitute arbitrary limits on muscles, seconds, view counts or words.
Lightweight tasks remain lightweight. Native technical controls without an
artistic unit retain their supported route; do not relabel art as a control.

## Execution and review

For every actual candidate, freeze and inspect before modifying a bound source.
Review the complete claimed region, worst revealing view/pose and adjacent
interfaces beside positive references and actually accepted inputs. Technical
transport/source preservation and artistic form remain separate verdicts.

Native component reviews record `cycle_disposition` (causal_control_only,
art_rejected, scoped_image_proof_pass) with an observation. An early failure may
stop the full matrix/clip and still gets a disposition. A failed component cannot
claim image-proof pass. Worker failures are technical events, not artistic tests.
One correction may have several component reviews; count the correction once,
and report artistic candidates separately from non-art slots.

Final unit review also records hypothesis result effective/ineffective/
inconclusive and dependency checks. Changed interfaces require explicit
revalidation; a stale or unjudged boundary cannot pass. A passed micro-unit is a
versioned proof with supported conditions, not permanent immunity from regression.
If its neighbor changes, reopen affected checks and integrate again.

Director313 remains full-speed audiovisual judge. Source/timed-frame checks can
reject a visible defect but cannot certify temporal fluidity or sound. A human
AV queue must not absorb known frame-visible failures. No new routine per-unit
Director handover is introduced.

## Retrospective response and effectiveness

Retain350's warning on the second distinct unresolved attempt, hold on the third,
immediate foundation/regression/reference-gap/evidence triggers and exhaustion
synthesis. For version2 units, responses additionally require:

- `scope_assessment`: exact unit digest; keep/split/merge/abandon and reasons;
- `causal_assessment`: reference, positive_target, representation, execution,
  integration, evidence, judgment or administrative category; method family;
  changed prediction and previous result.

Every newly admitted version2 intervention is flagged `effect_required`. Another
remedy for the same finding cannot be admitted until its predecessor has an
outcome-linked effectiveness disposition. The packet exposes only pending
required effects, not the entire historical intervention inventory.

`evaluate` can now name `admission_event` explicitly after a failed/unknown test
has reopened the gate. It verifies a subsequent, same-plan, unsuperseded outcome
for that requirement. `ineffective` needs a matching FAIL; `inconclusive` retains
the unresolved finding. `effective` additionally needs the current TESTING gate
and matching diagnostic/production PASS; worker or negative-control PASS cannot
close art. Disposition of an older failed test never closes a newer gate.

Legacy responses/admissions remain history without retroactively fabricated
evaluations. Policy migration preserves counters and opens reconciliation. Do
not mark all old failures effective merely to tidy the ledger. Actual human
acceptance still requires its exact source and scope, independently of requirement
names or raw historical PASS rows.

When the Director actually abandons a former scope (386 returns from the armored
donor), trusted `supersede_scope` can record SUPERSEDED_SCOPE for its old auxiliary
finding. It needs an exact source-bound Director decision naming the task/key
and reason; current requirement IDs cannot be retired. It is neither PASS nor
history deletion. Pending old tests get the same scope disposition, not a false
effectiveness result. Registered consumers cannot reuse such a retired proof
as qualified. A new relevant failure reopens the finding. Human-source meaning
is still the trusted agent's responsibility; a JSON file is not authentication.

## Lossless packet transport

Large packets first deduplicate shared response bodies. If still over the
unchanged60,000-character bound, shared trigger event IDs are dictionary-encoded:
`trigger_events386` stores each complete ID once; each gate retains its exact
ordered membership as indices, with its original digest. No trigger, finding,
instruction or event is filtered out. Use `retrospective.expand_packet_gates`
to reconstruct/verify before inspecting gates, and `gate_digest(packet,key)`
when preparing responses. Legacy small packets remain unchanged. Round-trip,
corruption and gate-digest tests qualify transport only, not model recall or art.
Oversized unique content still blocks; do not raise the bound or silently omit
obligations. Full history remains in the canonical event ledger.
