## Failed practical methods for this horse/goal/resource envelope

Original-surface rest projection/fairing, posed elastic fitting, neutral-preserving
weight changes, secondary pectoral articulation/contraction, dual-quaternion
interpolation, primitive-volume projection and broad direct sculpt did not deliver
the required connected chest/root anatomy in the accepted rear. Approximately118
charged inherited-horse forequarter slots include these methods and their setup/
controls. Retire this open-ended crafting approach as a production strategy;
do not turn that conclusion into “ARAP/DQ/skinning never work” or “agents cannot
animate horses.” This actor/pose/quality envelope is the qualified claim scope.

## Specific technical conclusions

- **Fitting an incorrect goal more accurately cannot construct anatomy.**244
  projected toward a volume before246visually rejected that volume. Judge the
  target's supported mass/attachment before a precise inverse, cache or embedding.
- **Rest space, a nominal neutral frame and raised poses are different tests.**
  A valid bind/rest mesh is not a natural283or339surface. Bind coordinate space,
  topology/region, pose/time and render profile to every measurement.
- **Intersections and anatomical quality are separate.** Zero crossings is a
  structural control, not a natural silhouette or muscle-flow verdict. Do not
  compare different poses/topologies' counts as one artistic progress series.
- **Valid skin weights are not correct tissue ownership.**80deforming bone groups
  and4nondeforming masks were confused at253. Actual use_deform/geometry membership
  matters; an arm-weight label can include a visually important chest connector.
- **Preserve the accepted behavior, not every internal implementation field.**
  Exact raw vertices, internal bone paths and broad ownership freezes can prevent
  reconstruction. Record invariant owner/provenance; changing visible accepted
  performance remains Director-owned.
- **Segmented upper-arm interpolation was not a sufficient cause.**223's rigid
  support still showed the shelf. Do not keep tuning that cause without new evidence.
- **The inherited neck/chin corrective values were inactive in248's tested poses.**
  Stored large key deltas alone did not explain the tested bulge. Do not revive
  this explanation merely because the file contains those shape keys.
- **A zero control must exercise the actual mechanism.** A path skipping edit
  mode cannot calibrate edit-mode drift. New BMesh/key/UV transport needs an actual
  roundtrip; software validation of arrays is not that test.
- **Topological locality is not physical locality.**260's two-ring fairing reached
  far enough to demand138.45mm of movement. Inspect edge lengths/region support;
  do not widen a30mmguard to admit the destructive method.
- **Boundary position constraints alone do not ensure a valid joined surface.**
  Compatible cut frames, registration, orientation and self-contact all matter.
  259exposed a late fold despite clean earlier steps;261used known clean side-order
  and a1.54mm bounded separation. This is a static graft result, not a muscle or
  moving-cloth solver.
- **Source-driver health must be current and specific.** Historical334invalid
  driver findings do not describe every later source.184has its own report.
  A healthy driver report still does not establish biomechanical/anatomical validity.
- **Surface changes invalidate contacts and consumers.** Recheck saddle, seat,
  reins, visible coat and action transitions on the final surface, not an obsolete
  pre-repair cage or the most flattering view.

## Procedural dos and don'ts

Do start with the actual audience goal, reference roles, intended camera/pace,
positive form and a finite falsifiable qualification contract. Do front-load a
representative visible proof and keep all critical interfaces in view. Do separate
engineering controls, native visual evidence, full-speed AV and Director acceptance.
Do preserve cumulative effort and retired family identity across micro-tasks/models.

Do not use reduced error, more tests, a new method label or completed retro forms
as proof of usable footage. Do not freeze an unqualified target indefinitely.
Do not iterate the local cause after a control has falsified it. Do not hide bad
structure with finish, armor, flattering views or an undeclared lower quality bar.
Do not spend on dense interpolation/final detail before a usable positive form.

Retrospectives need an outcome linked to the same visible feature across methods.
Formal evaluation now works, but384evaluations with zero effective whole-feature
closure did not solve anatomy. Keep the useful control results, retire the
unproductive production investment, and offer explicit alternative tradeoffs.
