## Five-step workflow

1. **Brief and scope.** One asset card: story function, shots/framing, recurrence, protected anchors, flexible dimensions, required motion, quality floor, reuse expectation and budget boundaries. Name the single uncertainty the next step resolves.
2. **Feasibility reconnaissance.** Inspect project inventory first; then a small number of genuinely different free/tool-native routes if needed. Check actual geometry, editability, rigging, rights and adaptation burden. No catalog-only endorsement, paywalled “free” assumptions or open-ended shopping. Recommend a route, including “none suitable.”
3. **Whole-result prototype.** Coarse but coherent complete assembly, simple material palette, target-format frame and cheap representative pose/movement. At most2–3 materially distinct design/realization choices where they answer a real question; not three mandatory variants or filters. For recurring leads, keep facial identity readable. No polishing one subsystem while major interfaces remain untested.
4. **Select and lock.** Director accepts one achievable production design and explicit deviations. Extract reference views from the accepted native, retaining original concepts and lineage. Technical review and creative acceptance stay distinct.
5. **Finish selectively and hand off.** Fix blocking defects, then high-value visible improvements; verify native/reopen, one local revision and required motion. Record accepted scope, known limits and next dependency. Stop optional polish and move to the shot.

## A concrete stopping and change policy

### Feedback triage adopted at exchange156

**Internal visual exit gate adopted at exchange161.** Before presenting a completed correction, record each known blocking defect, its accepted reference or required pose, the candidate image inspected and PASS/FAIL/NOT_RUN. Compare accepted versus candidate directly under matched camera, scale and light; rejected predecessors may diagnose a mechanism but cannot set the quality floor. Inspect normal review size and local crops, both sides and adjacent regions. For the current body, throat/clavicle shape must preserve the accepted anatomy; shoulders must remain coherent, and seated elbows, arm surfaces, knees and lower abdomen must not show gross rubbery bending, streaks, folds or skew. Any FAIL or NOT_RUN keeps the candidate internal. Technical controls have a separate gate and do not overrule visual failure. Do not waive a structural issue by promising clothes or calling the result YELLOW. If a method cannot meet the known target, bring a concrete method/authority decision with failed evidence and viable alternatives, not another cosmetic go-ahead. These are enforceable handoff conditions, not a new polish target or automatic Director acceptance.

**Continuation rule reaffirmed at exchange158:** when the agent substantially agrees with feedback and its correction fits the authorized goal, diagnosis feeds implementation in the same continuation. Do not yield a diagnosis-only final answer, commit-only milestone or request for cosmetic permission. Keep progress in commentary and review known failures internally. Stop mid-goal only for a genuine blocker or a decision requiring Director authority; completion is the normal handoff. This does not remove cumulative method reassessment, hard limits, protected-state checks or final creative acceptance.

Director observations are inputs to prioritization, not an automatic implementation checklist. Before rework, classify each observation as **fix now**, **reassess after the upstream correction**, or **defer until a named production test**. State the relevant shot/dependency, the smallest useful correction, what stays protected and the condition that would reopen a deferred item. Do not quietly drop observations, assume every comment requests perfection, or hide structural failures behind future clothes.

For the current body: head-to-body scale and shoulder/armpit construction block credible outfit fitting; gross rubbery arm bending blocks the seated reach screen. Correct those together. Reassess collarbone/neck prominence after coherent assembly scaling before sculpting accepted anatomy. A fine residual seam may wait for the actual neckline/drape and pose, but a gap, silhouette kink or visible neckline break may not. Detailed skin, hidden torso anatomy, muscle/finger polish and a universal rig are not prerequisites for that next step. This is application of existing Efficient Quality/forward-momentum principles, not a lower quality floor or new production objective.

An accepted component is not automatically an accepted assembly: preserve identity and source data while checking its scale, placement and interfaces in context. Conversely, local-data hashes cannot establish that the new assembly looks right. The next useful exit is a coherent foundation that can carry the planned clothes and seated reach, followed by a dressed riding candidate—not another isolated anatomy campaign.

- Classify issues: **blocking** (identity loss, visible intersections, broken deformation, rights uncertainty); **material improvement** (noticeably better intended screen result); **optional** (microscopic/off-camera detail or personal perfection). Rights uncertainty blocks release/use as applicable, not necessarily private diagnostics.
- Before another cosmetic pass, state the defect, relevant framing, proposed visible benefit and what remains protected. If no meaningful change is perceptible at delivery size, defer it unless it protects a known future shot or continuity dependency.
- Once blocking issues pass and the Director accepts the look, freeze it. Reopen only for a demonstrated use-case failure or explicit creative revision, not comparison with an unadopted reference detail.
- Three similar unsuccessful variants trigger a method/target reassessment, not another renamed pass. Existing20-net-minute diagnostic and60-net-minute overall checkpoints are **reassessment points**, not guarantees, per-asset caps or routine approval requests. The earlier unlimited-time override was for the hair capability exercise, not a blanket future production policy. Do not silently impose a new hard ceiling; agree scoped allowances before costly execution.
- Continue reversible in-scope work without routine go-aheads. Escalate only a material identity/style/plan change, new authority, hard-budget limit or genuine blocker. Bring actual evidence, alternatives and a recommendation.
- Separate capability R&D from production. A learning experiment may legitimately cost more; label it and state the learning goal/exit condition. Do not charge its entire discovery cost as repeatable per-asset runtime, or call later reuse free.
