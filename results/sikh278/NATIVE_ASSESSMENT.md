### Carry-forward learnings

1. Test geometry without texture: source image quality can conceal fused/missing functional anatomy.
2. A single posed portrait bakes pose and infers hidden surfaces. Future generation inputs should prefer neutral alignment and separable components when that serves the next use; changing input format is not a promise of better output.
3. Target generation at the strongest component. Successful turban mass recovery does not imply successful hair or facial performance.
4. Separate generation cost from conversion cost. One10-credit generation and a small GLB say nothing yet about repeatable cost-to-accepted actor.
5. Review-camera registration/light is evidence infrastructure: first overlit/torso-front views were inadequate and replaced without editing geometry.
6. Free-web export worked through the Director's manual download; the browser automation failure was not a Meshy paywall. Do not spend export quotas repeatedly when the transfer mechanism is failing.
