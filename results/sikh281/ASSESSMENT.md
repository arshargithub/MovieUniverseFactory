## Carry forward

1. **Approval continuity:** the previous handoff was unnecessary. The Director had approved the conversion proof, not merely the prerequisite download/inspection. Before ending a pass, compare the completed deliverable with the previously agreed end goal; an intermediate strategy note must not narrow that goal into a new approval boundary. Clarifications of existing authority are not new spending or creative permissions.
2. **Interfaces first:** source detail and polygon count do not supply performance topology. Separate the globe, lid travel and surrounding skin explicitly, then test partial closure before multiplying eyes, expressions or hair.
3. **Preserve the right boundary:** exact preservation of every vertex at a jagged surgical cut propagated defects into the new surface. A declared local blending zone was more effective than repeated offsets while protecting unrelated anatomy. Preserve the original donor and record the scope of local changes.
4. **Temporal geometry matters:** test interior interpolation states, not only open and closed endpoints. A shape key labelled blink proves neither coverage nor anatomical motion.
5. **Scoped success is not actor admission:** carry the mechanism forward, not this generic bald identity.
