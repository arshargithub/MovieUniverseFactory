## What this study can and cannot decide

It can support a Director discussion of approach/widening/obstruction, controlled angled stop, uncover/respect, mounted response, opening corridor and departure. The actual motorcycle's main geometry is usable for this scope, with separate rolling wheels, steering, low drag bar and rider support. The positive visual finding is source/handling suitability, not a claim that the one-material donor is a finished hero asset.

The faceless mannequins deliberately cannot settle identity, authentic finished turban folds, beard, facial performance, hand anatomy, garment dynamics or ensemble quality. Crop blades/tree masses/sky, dust intensity, surface treatment and night palette are provisional. Full-production admission remains BLOCK. A silent12fps study cannot establish final24fps motion quality, engine/hoof/sound impact, music compatibility or real-time audience response. Frame inspection and technical continuity are not represented as direct playback/listening.
