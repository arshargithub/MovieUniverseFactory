The web listing could not be freshly fetched during intake; embedded metadata and the prior listing inspection are the evidence, not a newly obtained release opinion.

Director supplied `Downloads/royal_enfield.glb`. The unchanged source is `.runtime/assets/series01-motorcycle289/royal_enfield.glb`. Intake found154 arbitrary export meshes, one material, no image textures or rig. Spatial seam welding and loose-component separation yielded755 connected parts; these are not755 independently functional mechanical assemblies.

Local changes: uniform normalization with a small pitch correction to level tire contacts; independent wheel and steering ownership; source mirrors/high bar and deployed stand removed in the derivative; low drag bar/grips added; muted military-olive, steel, rubber and saddle materials; light and authored motion. Original remains unchanged. No claim to depict an exact wartime restoration. No creator endorsement implied.

Horse source remains the existing local `demonstrator-01/horse.blend`; this study does not newly clear or publish that source. Character proxies, crop/landscape geometry, lighting and authored choreography are staging-only. Publication, actor adoption and final asset rights reconciliation remain separate gates.
