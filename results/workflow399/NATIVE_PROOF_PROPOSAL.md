## Immediate rejection and stop conditions

- License/export route becomes unclear or depends on payment: reject/park.
- Required chest/shoulder form cannot survive the rear without anatomical mesh
  rebuilding, another custom weight/sculpt campaign, armor concealment or reducing
  the accepted action: reject that candidate. Routine coordinate/control mapping
  and bounded technical transport fixes are allowed within the same ceiling.
- A failing required image remains failed; more joints, successful import,
  improved metrics or a flattering view earn no admission.
- No positive native form within the candidate allowance: stop its branch, retain
  failure evidence and move to candidate2only under the same shared allowance.
- Shared8exhausted: stop and present actual evidence plus a free-only creative/
  technical alternative. No automatic renewal, Astra escalation or paid fallback.

## Expert and review lenses

Anatomy: connected supported mass, ordinary and extreme poses, no shelves/webs.
Rigging/deformation: evaluated spaces, ownership, contact and trajectory fidelity.
Animation/biomechanics: bracing/power/hold/descent and continuous transitions.
Cinematography/look: inspect revealing forms at actual framing; preserve majestic
same-film presence, without treating textures as a substitute for geometry.
Production: frozen source, no variant explosion, meaningful proof before finish,
shared allowance, clear failure-triggered reallocation and unqualified interfaces.
