## Ranked leads

1. **abhayexe — Horse Rigged(Game Ready)**
   https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/horse-riggedgame-ready-bc64f4ff7966474ca9bacd42fa73a754
   Live page and Director-provided GLB both CC-BY4.0.7.60MB,42-joint skin,
   one60.33s action, one image/material, KHR_materials_unlit. Published preview
   actually inspected: useful coat detail, proportioned unarmored body and a
   plausible connected standing forequarter. Low polygon density and baked/unlit
   shading remain adaptation risks. No named rear; no mesh-deformation evaluation.
   Read-only numerical track audit did not establish a rear and must not be used
   as visual proof. Prefer first native qualification, not immediate adoption.

2. **ShakeySco — Realistic Horse Mesh (Rigged)**
   https://blendswap.com/blend/18045
   Free CC-BY listing,13.7MB native Blender2.7x. Creator reports corrected mirror/
   weight painting and unfinished texture. Published1024x576front-oblique image
   inspected via browser: readable connected barrel/shoulder mass, separate legs,
   dark unarmored body; one flattering view cannot prove deformation. Unknown
   modern constraints/driver health, exact downloaded license notice and ability
   to sustain the high rear. Login required for download; no paid tool advertised.
   Second candidate if1fails. Do not paint before source form/pose proof.

3. **Lyndon Daniels mesh / ChadM rig — Rigged Horse**
   https://opengameart.org/content/rigged-horse
   CC0 listing plus explicit upstream Realtime Rancher's model-pack link.
   Native.blend,20.2MB, anonymous download link. This is a separate rigged variant;
   the original static model/preview already served as an anatomy reference386,
   not as our live horse. Existing published preview inspected locally: stocky
   body, coarse silhouette/hair and dated texture. Page notes missing weights for
   hair/eyes/tail; rig author describes an early learning exercise. Weak fallback,
   not a studio-ready animal. First two are preferable.
