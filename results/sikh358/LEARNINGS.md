#358 — coupled tack and rein evidence

Status: construction lessons, not whole-character acceptance.

1. **Control names are not anatomical landmarks.** The inherited head-bone tail was treated as a mouth location. Actual mesh and bone inspection showed that it was above and behind the muzzle. Bind tack to measured surface anatomy, and explicitly name its owner for every consuming system.
2. **Review the full relationship, not just both endpoints.** Motion17 had zero computed endpoint error and preserved the hand span, yet the rendered rein entered the neck. Contact acceptance requires the complete visible path, both sides and action phases. Endpoint arithmetic is a necessary check, never the acceptance criterion.
3. **Do not preserve a defective intermediate path.** Preserving the old last quarter protected the grip, but blending an endpoint correction through the rest retained an incorrect central route. Preserve the actual owned contact and protected actions; allow an erroneous connecting span to be reconstructed.
4. **Nearest-surface projection has a topology failure mode.** Appearance84 projected crown leather onto the ear instead of the skull. Motion19 projected adjacent rein samples onto opposite sides of the neck, leaving a segment penetrating by about90mm. Prove surface ownership/connectivity, not just proximity of individual points.19 was rejected before save.
5. **Appearance clues require object evidence.** A tan-lit span was initially suspected to be duplicate reins or a material override.18 found exactly the two intended curves, DATA-linked materials and no modifiers/shape keys. The duplicate hypothesis was not supported; do not state it as the cause. The visible route failure remains independently established.
6. **Separate useful components from integrated qualification.**86's headstall is a retained construction improvement. It does not qualify the rider, horse action, rein contact, clothing or full-speed audiovisual performance. Keep unexecuted checks NOT_RUN and failures visible.
7. **Allowance additions must retain unused corrections.** Explicit five-per-task authorization extended existing limits without losing appearance82 or resetting original history. The helper now accepts a top-up at untouched GROUNDING only, never during active construction. Tests preserve authority and duplicate-event protections.
8. **Final sampling is part of the construction, not harmless export.**20's connected exterior path did not guarantee that resampling preserved its clearance.21 preserves path corners but still fails a dense clearance guard. Validate the exact curve that will be rendered, including its thickness, after interpolation/sampling. Closed-obstacle unit tests are useful negative controls, not substitutes for the actual animated horse.
9. **Account honestly for cycles.** This allowance includes two read-only diagnostic attempts and several rejected-before-save constructions. Report attempted cycles separately from useful visual candidates; do not imply a full completed appearance/motion pass from an exhausted counter.

Application points: planning should require actual anatomical ownership and consumer mapping; early proof should include visible connecting geometry and reverse/partial poses; technical review should evaluate animation after frame changes; visual review must reject visible penetration despite numerical endpoint success. Retrospective method changes remain source-bound to the original task and limits.
