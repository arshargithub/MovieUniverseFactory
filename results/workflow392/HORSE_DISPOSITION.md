## Could more micro-tasks have helped?

**Yes, a better proof decomposition could have reduced wasted work. It does not
justify resetting the failed family now.** Separate independently judgeable proofs:

1. One connected neutral forequarter: chest mass, lower-neck flow and both shoulder/
   upper-leg roots, in revealing multi-view context. Preserve this positive form
   as a versioned proof, not a mathematical fit score.
2. The same construction in one worst raised pose: determine whether binding/
   articulation preserves that form. Do not simultaneously change the goal,
   neutral shape and deformer without a discriminating control.
3. Descent/contact transitions: show that the tissue remains convincing between
   passing endpoints and does not create a new web, shelf or volume collapse.
4. Continuous original action and affected rider/tack/coat interfaces.

Do not split individual muscles or vertex masks into artificial independent wins:
their visible attachment is the problem. Every proof retains necessary coupled
context and shares the original parent budget. The earlier repeated “UnitA” label
did not prove that this decomposition existed. Nor would a newly named micro-task
make a retired original-surface fitting technique materially new.

This sequence should have guided the earlier investment. At the current point,
use it only to judge the already-built graft in the terminal probe or to qualify
a genuinely different sourced representation. It is not a reason to fund another
large anatomy-crafting campaign.
