# Nihang retrospective: preserve useful proofs through integration

Exchange348. Read-only causal audit; no new renders or production changes.

## Conclusion

**This retrospective was worthwhile because it changes the next construction
recommendation.** We did not merely fail to make a garment. Appearance23 produced
a useful neutral garment and controlled bend. Integration failed; the actor and
rig changed; correction35 replaced the garment with an evaluated body-shaped
shell to remove intersections. That improved coverage while sacrificing the
clothing form we needed. Later corrections largely refined that failing form.

The next proof should therefore isolate **anatomy/deformation and garment transfer**,
not simply repeat “make a neutral garment” or polish the latest scene. The existing
23 proof is reusable evidence, not an approved costume or a guaranteed fit to the
new actor. [Revised next proof](NEXT_PROOF.md) supersedes the incomplete method
recommendation in347, without changing its failure disposition or granting cycles.

## What the cycles taught us

### 1. Known lessons did not reliably change the next operation

339 already called for neutral garment construction before posed fitting.340
instead used straight-rest sleeve mathematics while still settling the complete
garment on bent bodies. Its own reassessment admits the substitution. The c13
challenge admitted work while acknowledging that the required neutral native
proof remained unproven.

The pattern recurred at48: the challenge explicitly predicted that relaxation
could produce a shrunken body shell or clipping, and said to repair the
representation if this happened. Actual48 failed; the remaining package still
continued variations of that shell. Writing the right warning was not enforcement.

This is primarily a **method-compliance and causal-testing failure**, not evidence
that the Director needed to describe the desired clothing more precisely. The
knowledge system can retrieve rules; this history does not establish reliable
application. More rules, more context or a different model are not demonstrated
solutions to that failure.

### 2. Integration discarded a useful result

23's garment is plainly unfinished, but its neutral volume and controlled bend
are useful.24 exposed integration failures on the earlier mixed actor.29–34 moved
toward a coherent body/rig and encountered support, skin and cloth problems.
35's body-derived shell solved much of the skin breakthrough while returning to
the wrong clothing silhouette. Coverage, stable contact and visual clothing form
were being treated as substitutes when they are separate requirements.

The reusable unit must include its **body, rig, coordinate frame, deformation
method, supported poses and known limits**, not just a mesh or last successful
save. Changing that interface invalidates the relevant proof. Preserve the useful
shape as a comparison while adapting it; do not silently replace it to satisfy a
narrow collision metric.

### 3. The motion branch produced real but narrow progress

The ownership investigation found broken drivers and mixed control mechanisms;
repairing those enabled more articulated poses. Preparation and landing still
needed separate work. Repeatedly modifying input animation curves did not ensure
the desired movement of the actual hooves.

Motion12 measured and corrected world-space arrival at the ground. Its preserved
report gives final downward speeds of approximately3.94 and3.61m/s and a maximum
target residual of about0.000020m. Those are useful checks on the specific
pre-contact slowdown, **not proof of natural whole-body rearing**. Reference
preparation, hindquarter support, rider response, absorption and real-time flow
remain separate obligations. Likewise, a dark coat or fine surface detail does
not by itself solve the horse's sculptural, rigid appearance.

Keep that trajectory result. Do not reopen it casually or promote it into a
complete performance pass. Some intervening motion cycles recovered/extracted
evidence rather than testing new animation ideas; counting each as another
creative attempt misrepresents the history.

### 4. Review containment improved more than production convergence

331 openly records that written stages were not actually submitted before native
construction. Later native receipts, prerequisite failures and final visual
rejections are stronger containment:52 is technically saved and reopened, yet
still rejected. That is better than presenting another predictable failure as
ready. It is not evidence that we are efficiently achieving the visual bar.

Small interface wins accumulated while the complete leader, horse appearance and
crowd remained unqualified. We need to measure **retained useful proofs and closed
whole-beat requirements**, not just tests passed, cycles completed or defects
prevented from reaching the Director. No accepted quality bar should be lowered.

### 5. Cheap implementation checks belong before expensive native work

There were wrong object names, coordinate/domain assumptions, stale bindings and
inventory problems as well as genuine artistic failures. Examples include the
missing saddle assumption at51, unintended body-region selections, and editing a
bound source during an active motion job. These do not establish a Blender or
agent capability ceiling.

Inspect the actual owning scene, semantic object/vertex membership and evaluated
transforms; freeze active inputs; test the smallest revealing poses before long
bakes. A successful save, reopen, checksum or numerical contact test remains
technical evidence only.

## What the numbers do and do not show

motion0–12; zero denotes the initial attempt and is excluded below.

- **Appearance:52 numbered corrections;30 have a RETURNED save receipt;22 do not.**

  can still fail later rendering, reopening or visual review.
- **Motion:12 numbered corrections;7 have a RETURNED save receipt.** Several
  others were preservation/extraction work, not new animation candidates.
- There is no whole-leader acceptance or complete isolated-passage acceptance
  in this campaign. Scoped progress must not be reported as either.

Raw cycle totals mix planning failure, implementation failure, evidence recovery
and artistic experiments. Future reporting should separate those stages, retain
the authorized counter unchanged, and track recurrence after a known diagnosis,
integration retention, and whole-requirement closure. No historical net-hours
total or percentage efficiency gain is asserted: incomplete and overlapping job
logs cannot justify one. This audit's own logged work excludes unmeasured initial
source recovery and is not a campaign-cost estimate.

prospective prevention or fewer Director inputs. That claim requires a subsequent
native proof and actual review.
