## Reusable controls

1. **Relocation must be reviewed through the shot.** Adding6m of stop clearance also changes approach, crowd establishment and every stop-relative camera. A first camera adjustment double-applied the translation because the old target already used the new travel function. Inspect actual keyframes plus a top-down clearance control; do not infer the perceived gap from a scalar distance. Reapply after actor/bike/crowd envelope changes.
2. **Increased detail can reduce credibility.** The first cloud warp looked swirled, old ruts looked painted and crop/stone triangles read as manufactured shapes. Use lower-frequency asymmetry, coherent field rows, curved narrow foliage and grounded irregular stones. Inspect low-camera foregrounds before duplicating detail across the entire landscape. Noise and polygon counts are not aesthetic metrics.
3. **Evaluate transforms before binding attachments.** A new bit endpoint captured its matrix before the dependency graph evaluated its location; the resulting rein stretched toward the world origin. It crossed unrelated camera shots. Update the evaluated state before preserving a world transform, assert plausible whole-assembly endpoint distances, then inspect partial/full horse poses. Numeric length checks catch this escape, not the quality of a rein grasp.
4. **Fit supports to actual geometry.** A guessed oval girth hung beneath the horse. Raycast its route against the actual body in the intended pose, while keeping ownership on the supported body pivot. That is tested-use fitting, not a general saddle or cloth simulator. Reopen on a new horse, deformation, pose or costume.
5. **Effects have births and persistence.** New dirt and fresh marks are born at evaluated slip contacts, then remain in world space with a low ballistic/gravity trajectory. Check onset, peak and dissipation; a correct static plume does not establish motion naturalness. Lighting, camera, lane height or travel changes invalidate relevant evidence. General tire forces and soil physics are not qualified.
6. **Reuse picture separately from editorial encoding.** Accepted Pashtun media is input to the join, not a Blender render dependency. Bind its exact source hash, preserve its unfaded join PCM, record the original music source offset and inspect the edit boundary. Container re-encoding is not a native rerender, but can change decoded pixels and must not be called bit-identical.
7. **Do not let secondary fidelity admit the lead by association.** A better road, crowd and horse does not qualify the previously rejected Sikh head or connected-cloth performance. Keep the principal proxy conspicuously labeled. The next character package must use the approved illustrated direction and same-film comparison to the accepted Pashtun; this scene's improved background is not a replacement acceptance target.
