# Sikh generic Meshy conversion assessment

The local conversion **fails the actor quality gate**. It recovered a separately editable crown study and demonstrated stored blink/head-turn controls, but did not produce an acceptable animated face, clean full facial topology, or a usable uncovering performance. This is a completed negative feasibility evaluation, not a character foundation awaiting the Director's tolerance.

## Why the conversion failed

The source is one connected static surface. It does not encode which vertices are skin, eyelids, beard roots, tied cloth, neck or shirt. However, the source alone is not responsible for these outcomes: the local implementation relied too heavily on coordinate masks, approximate registration, projection and height-based deformation weights. Those approximations were insufficient for the interfaces being tested. Incorrect loop correspondence and independent seam movement were implementation failures, not proof that Blender or Meshy cannot support the task.

The two approaches expose different work burdens. Retargeting onto clean topology needs reliable anatomical correspondence, not just nearest depth. Operating directly on the sculpt preserves its appearance better initially, but requires careful local topology, semantic segmentation and deformation ownership. Neither has yet been demonstrated as an economical animated-face route here. No comparison with a paid Meshy model or a controlled expert conversion was performed.

Against the approved Sikh and accepted Pashtun, the diagnostic remains conspicuously weaker in integrated eyes, natural beard construction and overall facial credibility. Neutral clay intentionally omits final illustrated surfaces; that omission does not excuse these form/interface failures. No protagonist identity or same-film pass is claimed.

## Carry forward before another conversion

1. **Separate generation targets by production role.** A detailed clothed, bearded bust hides the very anatomy and interfaces needed for acting. For another generated face test, use a neutral, straight-on, clean-shaven adult head with visible open eyes, ears and unobscured jaw/neck. Keep turban and beard separate. This improves observability; it does not guarantee separate eyeballs or animation-ready topology.
2. **Register landmarks before projecting surfaces.** Bind eye corners/centres, nose tip and wings, mouth corners, jaw and crown boundary in both geometries. Render the registration overlay and reject mismatched correspondence before any shape transfer. A camera-angle estimate cannot substitute for this.
3. **Test one eye as a complete interface.** Validate seam continuity, globe seating, lid thickness, half closure, complete closure and normal return before duplicating or grooming. Preserve outer-edge neighborhoods together. Do not interpret a shape-key value of1 as a closed believable eyelid.
4. **Use anatomical regions for motion ownership.** The chin belongs to the skull even when below part of the neck. Beard roots must follow the same evaluated surface as the underlying face; clothing needs its own support. Height alone is an inadequate partition.
5. **Retain useful components without promoting the assembly.** This crown may save primary-form work. Its lower edge, fitting, thickness, rights and continuous free-end route remain adaptation tasks. A separated sculpt is not a tied, releasable cloth.
6. **Stop the method, not the quality requirement.** The known failures were kept internal. Their recurrence justifies an input/representation reassessment, not another decorated candidate, a paid upgrade, or a claim that agent-only creation is impossible.

## Recommended next decision

Do not buy a Meshy plan on this evidence. Preserve the crown as an optional donor, retire the combined-bust facial conversion, and test a head-only input before deciding whether Meshy belongs in the facial pipeline. First require landmark correspondence and one clean blink on that input; only then spend effort on beard, turban fit and uncovering. A new external generation/upload is not executed under this local-only pass. The alternative is to reconsider a separate face-construction route; the earlier FaceBuilder spending decision remains declined and is not silently reopened.

This recommendation changes the input strategy, not the approved Sikh design. The release dependency remains the Sikh's supported motorcycle stop, reveal and greeting; no additional trailer gate is complete.
