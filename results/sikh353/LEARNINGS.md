The61 waist query intersected sleeves.63's torso-weighted polygon set reduced
maximum support radius from about549mm to184mm and removed projecting strips in
four actual reverse poses. The first62 predicate wrongly excluded hip-weighted
lower torso: semantic regions can have legitimate adjacent-bone weights. Preserve

## Treat assemblies as assemblies

65 narrowed the turban courses but omitted the underlying wrapped crown core.
The core became a smooth cap masking the courses.66 used explicit eight-part
membership and restored visible wraps. A positive list must include structural
support parts, not just names of obvious decorative surfaces. Unit tests and
assembly assertions now preserve this actual negative control. A complete list
does not itself prove cultural authenticity or attractive form.

## Form and surface remain different problems

Actual66 close/production views show that quieter shader settings and additional
fibers cannot certify a convincing face, garment or horse. A toy-like result
must remain failed even when it has more polygons, curves and named materials.
Review intended framing plus revealing controls; diagnostic fill differs from
production lighting. Do not use a highly magnified diagnostic as the sole reason
to polish, nor use small framing to hide obvious silhouette/interface defects.

## Contact is phase-specific, and grip belongs to material

Fresh original reference-2/3 inspection shows a planted cue followed by the spear
being carried off the ground during the rear.59's useful arm repair preserved a
world tip throughout;14 inherited it. A small tip-error score therefore protected
the wrong invariant. A hand can touch a shaft while sliding along its material.
Plan the contact states and the material grip station explicitly; do not extend
one phase's ground constraint through the whole action. The bounded15 correction
produced visible carried-pole and held-grip improvement in four front and ten
production samples, but its downstroke timing was still wrong.

## A known timing failure can recur at a different contact

15's spear tip slowed from0.938 to0.295m/s before ground contact. Its endpoint
and monotonic-height test missed the same pre-impact ease-out already rejected
in the horse's forefeet.16 changes only the downstroke profile and the actual

The negative velocity series is a regression fixture. This establishes a
specific timing correction, not perceived force, sound synchronization or
continuous-motion approval. Apply contact-phase tests to each participating
object, not only the original object that exposed the failure.
