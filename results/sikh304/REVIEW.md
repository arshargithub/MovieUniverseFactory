## What the correction addresses

The main action problem was not simply excessive lean. The rider, vehicle angles, travel speed, wheel rotation, support and dirt did not tell one coherent physical story. Whole-clock derivative checks exposed implausible acceleration/deceleration in inherited and initially revised curves. Corrected curves are integrated from bounded acceleration rather than forced through arbitrary position endpoints. This is shot-authored choreography, not a validated vehicle simulator.

| Director observation | Realization and review requirement |
|---|---|
|Rider leans rigidly with bike|Separate torso counterbalance and braking brace; re-solve shoulder/elbow/hand relationships. Counterlean is not a universal rule for every turn.|
|No convincing release between skids|Two slip intervals, first moderate lean, upright rolling recovery, restrained second lean,42-degree settled heading. Inspect every transition frame and speed derivatives.|
|Support foot unclear|Inside support boot descends only toward the stop; opposite boot stays on its peg. Measure actual boot geometry against shared road after all root transforms. No claim about a particular historic motorcycle's brake-control layout.|
|Takeoff lacks traction story|Brief moderate extra rear-wheel rotation and23cm authored side displacement, limited yaw, then increasing forward acceleration. No wheelie or prolonged burnout. Rebuild camera aim and contact-born dirt from the actual path.|
|Road looks artificial|Remove protruding gravel decoration; shared compacted-soil surface, fine grain and wheel-lane wear. Skid scuffs sit on the road rather than as a raised strip.|
|Roadside ditch is only a depression|Curved rooted grasses, intermittent sediment and irregular edges, continuous with the same soil. No permanent standing-water claim.|
|Thin crop strips and empty outer ground|Cultivated parcels extend beyond the roadside and are separated by uncropped access strips and slightly raised earthen cross-bunds.|
|Crop perfectly still|Rooted spatially phased breeze; fixed-camera24/48 control as well as production frames. This is authored secondary motion, not a botanical wind solver.|
|Poor trees|Replace blob crowns with branching silhouettes and discrete leaf clusters; remove orphaned legacy canopy meshes. Boundary trees remain generic, not a verified species model.|
|Leader hill shape/material|Asymmetric earthen bank with integrated vegetated slopes and protected leader plateau. Every-frame rays and actual launch frames check that the road-facing side does not hide the bike.|
|Horse rear worsened|Hindquarter compression and support, separately coordinated forelimb/hoof angles, poll response and return; rebind reins and spear interfaces after body motion. Review neutral/rise/apex/landing from side and shot camera.|
|Motorcycle bland|Material-specific lower dust, rubbed olive paint, worn leather retained and exposed-steel response. Judge in the moving practical light, not by node count.|
