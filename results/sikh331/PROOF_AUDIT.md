# Production proof331 — failed early proof, not a review-ready clip

## Outcome and evidence boundary

The initial native pose trial and two corrective variants did not qualify the
Nihang horse/rider pose family or supporting-cast appearance. No crowd replication,
continuous clip, final sound mix or Director audiovisual queue was produced.
The accepted Pashtun253 master and rejected320 source were not edited.

This is a negative production result. It is not evidence that agent-only animation
is impossible, that the source horse rig is incapable, or that another model would
solve it. Native inspection showed usable controls; their coordinated use remains
unqualified. A narrower, useful finding is that root-pivot/joint-angle tuning plus
small garment offsets did not produce the required integrated forms.

## What actually happened

The330 brief and new331 strategy were read, a fresh domain knowledge packet was
created for each job, and local reviewed Blender handlers produced six phase
renders per variant. However, **the four controller stages were not submitted
before native construction**. The written stages were not equivalent to executing
the controller. This is an adoption/admission failure, not successful prospective
qualification of330. The later controller record is explicitly retrospective
frozen-evidence review; it cannot repair that chronology or demonstrate independent
reviewer judgment. No separate runtime/model calls were made.

The controller currently supports offline and frozen-evidence modes only. It does
not own this Blender launcher or enforce the ordered pose/look prerequisites on
native jobs. A native-authoring bridge remains unqualified. The agent could still
have applied the stage API before diagnostic work and did not; the software limit
does not excuse that omission.

  small offsets, rather than the reference's gathered neck/poll/chest/quarter shape.
- **P2 drive/crest:** forehoof orientation is better at327, but the lower support
  remains comparatively extended and the forelimb folds abrupt. Changing head
  pitch did not establish a coherent spine/neck/support shape family. Full reverse
  support and visible hoof-contact proofs were not completed.
- **P3 contact/recovery:**350/353 show more compression. That alone does not qualify
  reaching transitions, absorption timing or rider/seat/stirrup/rein interfaces.
  Old dependent caches were not all rebuilt; this is NOT_RUN, not pending approval.
- **P4 appearance:** intended-size frames retain a cap-like crown, shell-like
  torso, narrow regular beard, tubular lower clothing and a polished horse coat.
  The rounded saddle pad is less slab-like, but its spine support is unproven.
  This fails the accepted film floor without requiring a full-speed AV judgment.
- **P5 crowd:** not propagated or newly qualified because the representative
  actor prerequisite failed. Existing320 crowd remains rejected.
- **P6 continuity:** only sampled phase frames, not the contiguous252–441 passage.
  Reverse controls, fresh-open render comparison and sound synchronization NOT_RUN.
- **P7 process:** late controller use, incomplete early recipe snapshots and
  inherited transitive handlers not all pinned in the job input list. pose03 has
  source snapshots for its listed code; pose01/02 do not. Do not claim exact replay
  recipes or a validated authoring bridge for these trials.

## Correction accounting and stopping

The331 strategy inherited a two-correction package allowance. Its wording that
early diagnostics are not external reviews must not become an unlimited retry
loophole: these were three corrective variants of the same construction hypothesis.
Count them as initial attempt plus two corrections. The retrospective frozen
review records those three actual variants; it does not grant three more attempts.
The native launcher is closed for this package. No elapsed-time limit is the
reason for stopping. No knowingly failed candidate is sent for artistic review.

## Consequence and recommendation

Do not scale these forms into a crowd, add more random movement, or render the
whole passage. Preserve the visual goal and accepted Pashtun floor.

Recommended next package: qualify a **local native-authoring admission path**,
then perform a representation-level proof rather than another offset pass. The
bridge must bind original sources/stage receipts, allow only scoped native handlers,
own the single-job lease and shared correction counter, stop dependent work after
a failed pose/look prerequisite, and feed actual candidate bytes into fresh review.
Use the current chat for reasoning; no paid runtime calls, new models or cloud are
needed merely to implement that boundary. This is a proposed scope change from
the failed native proof, not a feature claimed implemented here.

The subsequent construction must solve hind support, pelvis/chest/neck/poll and
rider contact as one pose family; rebuilding the visible garment/tack forms is
separate from microtexture. Exit: reference-comparable gather/crest/contact poses
and one same-film supporting actor at intended framing, with reverse/contact and
transition evidence, before crowd propagation or the integrated sequence.

Remaining alternatives are to continue manual main-chat authoring with a clearly
declared weaker enforcement boundary, or to qualify the bridge first. The latter
is recommended because this proof repeated the exact execution-versus-procedure
gap that the Director asked330 to address. No change of creative quality, paid
asset purchase or specialist is proposed.
