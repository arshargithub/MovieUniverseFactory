## Discriminating controls and learning application

- **Component usefulness is not whole-character viability.**281's eye closure proof established an editable interface, not detailed hero eye anatomy. Before future extensive grooming, require complete face/crown/groom primary forms to satisfy the co-star comparison. This pass still spent too much work decorating a weak whole-head result; rejecting the handoff corrects admission, not that upstream sequencing failure.
- **Root cause versus appearance:**10's magnified eye view exposed the broad pupil reflection.11 reduced it and restored visible brown iris/pupil distinction, but the remaining schematic eye region did not pass. A resolved shader defect must not erase the unresolved anatomical/design verdict.
- **Groom hierarchy:** adding density, randomized paths or shorter cheek growth can produce wool/frizz, not natural designed locks.10 demonstrates this regression.12's length-scaled curl improves coherence but does not establish a convincing beard. Do not carry these guide parameters forward as an accepted groom preset.
- **Texture is not shape:** two built-in generated surface-paint studies were tested locally.01 added natural-looking variation;02 made illustrated marks explicit. Neither supplied the missing whole-character form. Preserve them as diagnostics, not new approved images or UV/albedo production assets.
- **Pose and transform ownership:** source objects may carry parents/inverse transforms; keyed local Euler channels can erase attachment compensation. Record rest/world spaces and fresh-reopen intermediate states before long rendering. Current baked skull attachment is scoped to this short study, not a reusable full actor rig.
- **Source constraints matter:** the intentionally generic free sculpt exposed anatomy and reduced fused-beard problems, but did not supply the desired individual design. This is a failure of this adaptation route, not proof that Blender, Meshy, MPFB or agent-only production is universally incapable.

These lessons are linked from the active strategy and release plan. A future head packet must bind this report alongside271. They do not automatically enter the curated pilot graph; manual reconciliation remains required. No source pins were refreshed to bypass review.

Preserve useful crown/eye/transform controls, but do not protect their current appearance as accepted.

If the source gate fails, close that route before actor integration. No subscription or upload performed here.
