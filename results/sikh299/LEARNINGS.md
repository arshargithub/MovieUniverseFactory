## Contact is three separate contracts

1. **Kinematics:** axle radius is insufficient when a tire has tread width and the bike steers/leans. Measure the rendered tire's support geometry against the actual road at every frame; solve both supports rather than lowering the bike by eye.
2. **Representation:** confirm evaluated versus raw mesh, used versus orphan vertices, modifiers and coordinate spaces. Here the road transform is identity and the tire has no modifiers; these are inspected facts, not general assumptions for a future asset.
3. **Audience readability:** correct geometry can still appear airborne under broad multi-light fill. The side diagnostic and real camera failed despite approximately0.8mm numerical gaps. Contact markers discriminated the cause. Localized, feathered contact occlusion is an explicit stylized rendering solution; it is not proof of physics and must not become a black disk or remain when airborne.

Apply this to feet, hooves, wheels, props and cloth support before sending a contact pass. Reopen after changes to light, camera, road, tire, lean or actor scale.
