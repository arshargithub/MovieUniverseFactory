do not create another service or new live LLM session. Require these compact

2. **Proof contract:** purpose-qualified references, positive features, known-bad
   controls, worst view/pose first, final coverage, explicit failure and downstream

   failed alternatives already tested, the smallest discriminating prediction,

   adequacy, causality, interfaces and protected quality. Multiple independent

   needs return to Grounding; not every failure calls for new references.

The machine can enforce fields, bindings, dispositions, dependency and allowance
consistency. It cannot count artistic problems in prose, determine correct animal
anatomy, or guarantee an honestly phrased scope challenge. Keep the current

Avoid arbitrary limits on views, muscles, lines or seconds as a scope heuristic.

- Is the unit still independently judgeable? Keep, split or merge, with reasons.

  hash alone is not a new strategy. Record changed prediction or justified control.
