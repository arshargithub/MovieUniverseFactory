## Root causes that the next strategy must address

1. **A negative target dominated construction.** “Remove the bulb/web” repeatedly
   produced a flat panel, hanging lobe, shelf or another bulb. Required next proof:
   an explicitly described positive chest/shoulder form beside real references,
   before more fitting, caching or coat detail.
2. **Shape and deformation were solved together without enough separation.**
   134–136 reproduced the chosen target more precisely while still failing its
   appearance.145/146 moved the whole pectoral region with the limb and made
   paired masses. Rest shape, anatomical attachment and pose response need
   distinguishable hypotheses; they still require integrated visual tests.
3. **Scope was declared small more often than demonstrated small.** A single
   string called `primary_outcome` can encompass many independent failures.
   Actual `work_units.validate` accepts a deliberately absurd combined chest/
   neck/legs/tack/leader/crowd outcome when the other fields are valid. The saved
   negative control demonstrates this semantic gap, not actual admission or a
   universal failure of the controller.
4. **An admitted causal story became permission for another variant too easily.**
   Rejected interventions generally reopened findings, but formal effectiveness
   evaluations were omitted. `admit` also resets the local repeated-attempt
   streak; that is legitimate for a genuinely new causal test, but not evidence
   that another gain/weight setting is a new strategy. Cross-intervention
   recurrence and discriminating predictions must remain visible.
5. **Proofs did not always retain their useful form at integration.** This is
   explicit in348's garment history and369's successful deeper load exposing a
   saddle failure. A passed region is a versioned input with interfaces and
   supported poses—not a permanently closed checklist row.
6. **Technical overhead consumed the same scarce allowance.** Wrong proxies,
   dependency omissions, bounded packet failures and source/review ordering
   mistakes were real. Calibrated cheap controls, reviewed handlers and scoped
   source selection belong before expensive artistic attempts. Keep the existing
   consumed counts; do not refund or redefine the past.
7. **The documentation can disagree with deployed code.** 369's manual-only
   appearance description was superseded by374 implementation but remains in a
   mandatory guide.385's initial commentary repeated that stale description,
   then corrected it on reading live code. An adoption record and its entry-point
   guide must be updated together. This is not evidence that a new knowledge
   service or larger context would itself solve anatomy.
