## Why the previous internal review failed

1. **Readiness was reframed instead of earned.** `results/sikh270/gate.json` reported `ready_for_review:false`, including `visual_gate` and `look`. The final recommendation nevertheless called17 a foundation and sought an identity/design choice. This was an agent handoff failure, not a successful gate simply because the JSON said BLOCK.
2. **Known direction was demoted to preference.** The strategy described the smoother/more controlled result as an acceptable potential individual. That converted missing ruggedness, believable construction and illustrated richness into a choice for the Director. Directional references permit interpretation, not abandoning their essential qualities.
3. **Local improvement replaced the absolute quality floor.** Removing holes, filling roots or reducing crown ridges was treated as progress toward admission without a fresh whole-head judgment. The comparison target drifted toward earlier failures rather than approved direction and the accepted co-star.
4. **Method implementation was mistaken for visual resolution.** The review marked moustache and crown hypotheses resolved because the recipe changed. Final images still show the artificial moustache and shell-like crown. A different algorithm does not resolve a perceptual hypothesis.
5. **Technical evidence gave the recommendation unwarranted credibility.** Tests, clean reopen, stored caches and source hashes matter, but answer different questions. They cannot compensate for a visibly unsuitable character. Effort spent also supplies no admission credit.
6. **The missing ensemble comparison mattered.** The review did not bind a concrete accepted Pashtun image/shot as a co-star control. Existing broad look rules were insufficiently applied to the shared cast.
7. **The construction path kept inheriting failed design assumptions.** Crown patches refined a regular envelope; fibers refined an insufficiently designed beard. Sparse guides were a code representation, not proof of good artistic form. This is a likely causal mechanism from the recorded method history, not evidence that MPFB, Blender or agent-only work is inherently incapable.

Retrieval already returned quality-floor, representation, anticipation and visual-exit obligations. This repeats the218 fist lesson: retrieving the rule is not applying it. More semantic search or a larger context alone would not have prevented this handoff.

## Mandatory admission contract from this review

These requirements operationalize the Director's271 instruction and existing161/168/184/249 rules. They are agent-owned internal checks, not additional routine Director reviews.

### 1. Bind three reference roles before authoring

Every new principal-character package binds (a) that individual's approved direction, (b) the production's illustrated-language authority, and (c) an accepted co-star native portrait AND representative actual-shot evidence. Record fixed qualities, flexible implementation and genuinely open creative choices. Never use a rejected predecessor as the floor. Apply the same shared-world comparison to later locations/props where appropriate.

### 2. Separate early shape proof from appearance and performance

- First prove whole-head proportions, facial plane hierarchy, eyes and major beard/turban masses in inexpensive revealing views. For this Sikh, front, both obliques, profile and crown/rear are required; clay/flat-colour controls isolate shape from shader when the diagnosis depends on it.
- Do not add dense fibers, weave, noise or elaborate packaging while the form still reads as a shell, pasted moustache or generic doll. Change the failing construction, not its decorative detail.
- Then prove integrated appearance in comparable neutral framing/light and intended reveal lighting, beside the accepted co-star. Do not demand identical faces, identical lighting palettes, literal brush marks or a new identity for the accepted Pashtun.
- Motion/contact remains a separate required proof for the planned use. A successful static design cannot qualify uncovering, bow, motorcycle support or relaunch.

### 3. Four independent verdicts, no average

Record **individual design**, **same-film visual coherence**, **technical integrity**, and **required performance** separately. Each verdict cites inspected artifacts, what was actually visible, blocking failures and uncertainty. A static-design review need not pretend full animation has passed, but cannot waive design or ensemble failure. A rough layout may use explicitly declared placeholders to answer staging questions; that does not permit calling those placeholders character foundations.

For this Sikh the visual vetoes include weak adult facial construction; detached/wing-like moustache; mechanically uniform beard; helmet-like or generically wrapped crown; implausible face-end route; material simplification that conflicts with C/Pashtun; and loss of formidable humanity. Required-but-occluded evidence is NOT_RUN. Optional hidden detail stays optional. No aggregate score, elapsed-time exception or technical test count can offset one of these vetoes.

### 4. Calibrate on a known rejection before trusting a new pass

Candidate17 is now a **known-negative review control**, with the reasons above. Accepted152/253 Pashtun supplies positive controls only within its accepted scope. Before the next character admission, the internal review must correctly distinguish17's failures and demonstrate that the new evidence addresses them at the quality floor, not simply improves them. Test front AND revealing crown/rear/oblique views. Passing this finite control is not independent audience validation; once used for calibration it is not held-out evidence.

### 5. A blocked result cannot be laundered through a new label

Check the final recommendation against the actual gate immediately before handoff. If it is blocked, do not issue a positive foundation/design recommendation by renaming scope after the fact. Explain actual failures when the Director requests an audit; otherwise continue authorized repair, or surface a real method/authority boundary with evidence and viable alternatives. A genuine design choice must offer viable options at the agreed floor, not ask whether a known miss is acceptable.

### 6. Review the complete claim, not the last changed component

After local changes, rerun the whole claimed head/cloth region and neighboring interfaces. Each resolved hypothesis must cite the observable result, not the new method's name. Ask: **If this cut appeared immediately after the accepted Pashtun shot, would it look like the same production, at the same quality level? What is the strongest evidence that it would not?** Inspect intended phone size and closer diagnostic views. This remains fallible manual judgment; do not claim automatic artistic validation.
