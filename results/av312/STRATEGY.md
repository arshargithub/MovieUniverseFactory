in context311. No production changes, new provider calls/uploads/spend, installs,

## Path and earliest proof

1. Recover307/311 state and inspect available media interfaces. Try a short
   existing audio excerpt through the available tool audio return. A player or
   a successful encode does not prove model hearing. No direct input means the
   review channel fails before artistic qualification begins.
2. If direct motion/audio input is unavailable, retain false capability flags.
   Do not rerun familiar image comparisons and rebrand them as a new review
   capability. Identify an implementable alternative and exact authority needed.
3. Prepare a small local, source-bound control set and hidden-answer protocol
   for the next route. Use accepted/rejected clips plus slowed, desynchronized
   and silent derivatives; no source masters are overwritten. These are test
   fixtures, not new creative treatments or complete quality benchmarks.
4. A future external route must demonstrate content access and distinguish
   positives/negatives with timestamps, specific observations and disclosed
   misses before it influences a production gate. No capability flag changes
   based merely on a provider supporting a MIME type.

plus alternatives/recommendation and required authority. This authorizes local
