## Test protocol and exit criteria — before any future call

1. Keep prompts neutral and independent: describe observable events/timing first,
   then assess the stated fast/heavy action intent. Withhold labels, known defects,
   transformed filenames and answer key. Never ask it whether the Director is
   right. Report uncertainty and unavailable modalities explicitly.
2. Six requests: rear pairC01/C02; timing pairC03/C04; sync pairC03/C05; sound
   pairC03/C06; debrisC07; terrainC08. Randomize A/B order locally and bind it.
   Full-rate/explicit-rate short input is required; Google's default1fps sampling
   can miss fast action. Verify the effective setting and reject silent fallback.
3. Require timestamps and specific content evidence. The delayed-sound control
   must report direction and approximate magnitude, not merely “something feels
   off.” Silence must not produce invented hoof/music/neigh observations. Slow
   copy must be identified relative to approved timing. Rear review must identify
   actual coupled-motion differences rather than generic horse advice.
4. A false pass on a known critical negative, invented sound on silence or failure
   to separate unchanged/changed controls fails that modality's gate. Track false
   positives too: do not reject the accepted excerpt just because it is stylized.
   Terrain/debris must be localized in actual footage, not guessed from prompts.
5. Even success qualifies only a narrow external-review workflow. It does not
   turn this agent into a direct listener, certify all future animation, or grant
   production release. Keep provider-authored observations separate from my
   frame/source analysis. Amend the review contract deliberately to represent
   external qualified evidence if proven; do not flip personal direct_* flags.
6. Before routine production admission, challenge the route with an unused
   natural defect/positive pair when available. The six-call pilot cannot alone
   establish general unseen-defect reliability. Report misses, not accuracy
   percentages from a tiny heterogeneous sample.
