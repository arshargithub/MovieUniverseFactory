surface. This does not adopt another horse identity, replace accepted action,
bring armor into the film or lower the Pashtun quality floor.

Candidate: Lyndon Daniels, Realtime Rancher's horse. Original author submission:
https://opengameart.org/content/realtime-ranchers-3d-model-pack
The original source explicitly declares CC0 and provides horse.zip/OBJ/Blender.
The later rig contribution also declares CC0 and credits the same author:
https://opengameart.org/content/rigged-horse
The source preview shows integrated shoulder/pectoral/foreleg volume; low game
resolution and texture quality are NOT the film target. Only geometry is being
considered as a spatial anatomy guide. Existing physical photographs and
Director video retain realism/action authority.

Rights/route before trial: CC0 source declaration permits commercial adaptation;
no provider model/weights/export dependency. Local numeric OBJ parsing through
existing Python/Numpy, then the already reviewed data-only native target route.
No untrusted Blender/script/asset code executed. ZIP is inspected and only
expected inert OBJ data extracted if safe; no extraction traversal. Textures,
rig actions and materials are not being adopted. No upload or private data leaves
the workspace. Acquisition is free; no paid call, install, subscription/cloud.

Risk: another low-resolution model may not supply a better chest. Inspect actual
OBJ shape against photos before allowing it to define a target; availability or
license is not anatomical qualification. Source pose/scale must be registered;
no blind nearest-point transfer or claim that third-party rig solves our action.
