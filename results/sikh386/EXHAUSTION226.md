## Lessons applied forward

1. **Stress-test the source construction early.** A plausible standing horse or a qualified file export is insufficient. Before investing in surface finish, prove the actual topology/rig in the most revealing required pose and adjacent partial poses.
2. **Freeze the approved behavior, not every implementation field.** Record whether an invariant comes from Director intent, a real consumer dependency, an agent method choice or a calibrated numerical guard. Primary timing/contact/performance stays protected; secondary tissue mechanisms may need to change. A method-owned constraint must not quietly become a Director-owned creative requirement.
3. **Require a positive form before precision fitting.** Another close inverse, regularized surface or valid cache is not progress when its visible target is wrong. Preserve a negative source as a control, not an acceptance standard.
4. **Track feature closure across interventions.** Renaming a method, changing a mask or admitting a new retrospective response does not close the same lower-chest/root failure. After controls rule out a cause, stop tuning that cause.
5. **Treat connected interfaces as part of the unit.** Chest mass, shoulder attachment and skin-fold behavior cannot always be solved independently. Conversely, a technical source control should not masquerade as an artistic candidate. Scope must be demonstrated through the proof, not just declared small.
6. **A zero control must exercise the relevant mechanism.** The early zero-strength support path skipped edit mode and therefore could not explain edit-mode drift. The later no-added-bone roundtrip did. Validate the measurement path before interpreting it.
7. **Retrospectives need constructive capability, not just enforcement.** The workflow can retain failures and require comparisons. It cannot guarantee that a plausible written causal story, expert-lens label or extra context produces correct anatomy.

These findings are linked from the mandatory386guide. That provides a retrieval path; it is not a claim that a new retrieval service or automatic artistic judge has been built. Future work must read this closeout and reconcile its source bindings before native admission.
