## What failed in our approach

The previous package recommended reconstructing the garment as clothing. This
package instead selected and continued relaxation, folds and finish on the same
body-shell construction. Its own strategy explicitly warned against spending
the allowance polishing a failing representation. We did not apply that warning
early enough. This is a strategy and execution failure, not a need for another
checklist or more Director descriptions of the same visible defects.

The unclothed reverse controls expose a hard, wedge-like elbow/upper-arm form
under the problem sleeve. The clothing is also independently unsuitable: inflated
shoulders, deep sleeve pockets and surface mottling do not read as constructed,
weighted fabric. A richer beard does not compensate for a generic face and eyes,
shell-like turban, unresolved clothing or unfinished horse and saddle.

Source inspection of the custom arm solve finds upper-arm segments oriented by
their long axis with a shortest-swing rotation. Unlike the forearm, the upper arm
does not receive an explicit transported roll target. This is a **diagnostic
hypothesis**, not proof that roll alone causes the deformation; rest anatomy,
weights and pose must be separated with matched controls. Earlier roll-only
correction46 already failed, so repeating that assumption would be unjustified.

## Recommended next package: anatomy and real garment construction

Do not spend another allowance on cosmetic variations of this shell. Retain the
accepted masters, useful attachment/contact work and every failed candidate.
Use the same existing assets and local Blender route, without new spend or tools:

1. Compare neutral and mounted bare-arm controls, including the rest mesh,
   joint frames and skin weights. Resolve the actual shoulder/elbow cause while
   preserving plausible shaft contact. A valid wrist metric is insufficient.
2. Only after that prerequisite passes, build an independent garment with
   deliberate shoulder/sleeve junctions, elbow ease and a supported skirt. Prove
   its neutral and mounted forms before any shader or decoration work.
3. Finish the complete mounted actor against the co-star floor, then qualify
   the isolated rearing/crowd passage. Do not propagate failed leader geometry.

Exit requires believable bare anatomy, garment form without known pinching or
penetration across front/reverse/contact controls, and a whole production view
that belongs with the accepted Pashtun. Motion/audio remain separate, with
Director313 audiovisual judgment. If the new construction cannot pass its early
proof, stop with that evidence rather than consuming the remainder on texture.
