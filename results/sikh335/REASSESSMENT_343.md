## What worked and what did not

The review gate withheld known defects. That is better than passing incremental
improvement, but it does not demonstrate an efficient construction process.
Two corrections were consumed by geometric-support assumptions before any
candidate existed. Unit tests and typed operations did not establish that those
assumptions held in the actual garment. The complete-actor approach also exposed
costume, attachment and performance interfaces that were not adequately proven
before baking all 190 frames. In33, fitting the belt and sash to the coat's rest
surface did not preserve their clearance in the posed result. They receive
independently approximated skin weights; the coat additionally has subdivision
and shrinkwrap. Therefore rest-space support and shared armature ownership are
not sufficient layer-coherence tests. This is a specific construction-interface
failure, not a missing rule about inspecting intersections.

The motion method still adjusts joint channels indirectly. A fast-changing
channel is not proof that the actual hoof accelerates into contact: body rotation
and limb folding combine at the endpoint. Future qualification must measure the
world-space contact trajectory, not infer it from channel intent.
