# Mounted leader construction lessons

## Regional support must include the evaluated surface

The waist and shoulder require different support regions. Resolving all 1503
surface samples before the animation solve avoided the missing-ray failures from
the previous pass. A complete rest binding still did not prevent the coat from
breaking through the belt after subdivision and deformation. The waist repair
uses a restricted, weighted coat support surface after deformation, plus a welded
angular seam. Actual front and reverse controls at frames 283, 327 and 350 show
continuous coverage in candidate45. This is not an all-frame cloth qualification.

Apply this before fitting belts, sashes or other close layers. Inspect the actual
modifier stack and semantic region; a global nearest surface or larger offset is
not a substitute for a valid support relationship.

## Attachment includes persistence dependencies

Matching quaternion mode and action slots stopped the restored beard cards from
detaching. Appending the cards also imported an unused source rig: save/reopen
dropped that orphan, changing the snapshot object count. Remove only newly
imported, unused dependencies before the immutable snapshot. Do not weaken the
reopen comparison to ignore the mismatch or remove unrelated source objects.
Candidate44 retains the failed admission despite producing three images;
candidate45 saves and reopens without that discrepancy.

## Contact requires a coupled anatomical solution

Candidate45 reaches the spear but folds the wrist unnaturally. Copying a wrist
bone's local X into forearm bones ignores their different rest frames.46 corrects
that error but still visibly fails: hand azimuth and elbow placement were being
chosen independently.47 instead uses the shoulder-to-shaft direction and a
reachable elbow minimizing wrist bend. Its close views remove the most obvious
wrist pinch, but its reverse views reveal a deep sleeve/elbow pinch. Recorded
wrist angles still range from 0.69 to 53.28 degrees across the 190 sampled frames.
No anatomical or garment PASS follows from the optimization.

Future contact solves must consider joint limits, torso clearance, garment
deformation and performance silhouette together. Preserve a revealing reverse
view, not just fingertip errors or a front image. The endpoint and rest-roll
tests are useful negative controls; they cannot judge plausible skin or cloth.

## Failure detection is not construction effectiveness

The process prevented a predictable bad Director review, but the five corrections
did not produce a same-film leader.43 failed at Blender startup;44 failed reopen
admission;45 and46 exposed unresolved anatomy;47 reveals the coupled garment
limitation. No measurable efficiency improvement is claimed. The accepted
Pashtun comparison still shows a substantial gap in face, eyes, turban, clothing
and saddle. Crowd propagation and complete passage rendering remain blocked.

The next proposed package should prove a complete leader in the actual mounted
shot, with the reverse and extreme poses as early constraints. Keep the useful
regional bindings and attachment repairs; replace the body-shell garment method
where it produces inflated shoulders and pinched sleeves. Do not spend another
allowance perfecting isolated contact metrics while treating the whole actor's
appearance as a later cosmetic phase. Execution needs renewed correction authority.
