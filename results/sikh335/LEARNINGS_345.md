# Mounted actor transfer and support lessons

## Preserve complete visual components

The33 transfer selected fine beard strands but omitted the fitted beard and
moustache base meshes. Asset extraction must verify the complete visual component
and its material, transform and animation dependencies, not just name-prefix
matches. The39 restoration exposed another dependency: the object's rotation
mode must match its copied quaternion action. Apply this before transferring
hair, facial attachments, costume hardware or crowd variants. Validate rest and
posed silhouettes; the42 mode repair has not yet been rendered.

## Distinguish mask groups from deform bones

Body meshes include groups such as Left, Mid and body that are not bone weights.
They must not enter deformation ownership sums or normalize actual influences.
The new pure regression excludes them. Garment support is region-specific:
a belt belongs to waist/pelvis support, not nearby thigh or arm skin. A shoulder
sash needs the actual shoulder region and cannot use an overly restrictive global
torso whitelist. Before expensive animation, preflight all surface samples and
report failed row, region and owner.40/42 failed because this was not proven first.

## Review the whole contact chain

Matching the spear axis to anatomical MCP rows improves grip readability, but
the close-up exposed an unnatural wrist/forearm transition. Hand orientation must
be coordinated with forearm roll and sleeve fit. Add a dedicated camera when the
whole-actor view crops the disputed interface; cropped evidence is NOT_RUN.
Actual41 hand frames support the diagnosis;42's repair has no rendered proof.

## Freeze active inputs

bound strategy during the job. The original bytes were restored; the failure
was preserved and counted. Put new observations in a separate unbound append-only
finding record until the job and its reviews close. No blanket rehash or false
successful receipt. Apply this to plans, trusted handlers and bound evidence.
