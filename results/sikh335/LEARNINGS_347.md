# Carry-forward lessons: replace the failing representation

## Diagnose below the garment before modifying it again

The final bare-arm controls expose an elbow/shoulder deformation concern beneath
the failing sleeve. A garment-only loop could not discriminate this. For future
mounted actors or bent limbs, pair neutral and extreme bare-anatomy controls with
the clothed view at the first structural proof. If anatomy fails, return to its
construction/pose/weights stage; do not hide it with cloth, shadow or framing.
Do not call the current upper-arm roll hypothesis a verified cause.

## A method warning must change execution

The347 strategy explicitly said not to polish a failing representation, but the
work continued largely as relaxation, folds and materials on a body-shell coat.
The final image remained predictably unsuitable. More written gates would not
have fixed this. At the first failed whole-form proof, compare the method actually
implemented with the required construction, and replace it before surface work.
Keep a discriminating early proof: if smooth, untextured form is wrong, adding
grain cannot make it right. Gates prevent premature delivery; they do not create
the missing modeling skill or guarantee convergence.

## Scene inventory and ownership precede modifications

Inventory revealed overlapping old and restored beard contributors. Hiding the
superseded sets and creating one skin-rooted, correctly head-attached assembly
improved continuity. By contrast, correction51 assumed a saddle object from a
different derivative and failed before output. Resolve exact semantic targets
from the actual source inventory before a bound worker launch. A source name
remembered from another scene is not an interface contract.

## Regional fixes still need complete-figure controls

Global corrective relaxation48 pulled clothing into skin; restricting it49
reduced that regression. Fold relief also disturbed the belt, requiring a waist
exclusion50. These are useful negative controls, not proof the coat is sound.
Front-only beard or seam improvements must not obscure reverse sleeve failure.
All new curves, seams and material additions remain rejected derivatives until
their parent assembly passes the relevant use.

## Preserve scope and honest evidence

Skin grain reused only a featureless region of an existing authored atlas; no
new generated image or identity transfer was needed. Static controls do not
qualify fluid rearing, sound or crowd behavior.26 tests qualify code properties,
not an artistic result. Five corrections produced three intermediate visual
rejections, one implementation failure and a final whole-actor rejection.
No efficiency improvement or fewer Director inputs has been demonstrated.
