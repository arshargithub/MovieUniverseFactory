# Construction-interface findings —319

Status: supported local observations from the authorized supporting-cast pass;
not Director artistic acceptance, a generally qualified rig, or new authority.
Evidence: `results/sikh319/REVIEW_PROGRESS.md`, `CARRY_FORWARD.md`, versioned
source snapshots and the exact native/render/check artifacts cited there.

## Apply before adaptation or construction

Bind semantic frames as well as transform matrices: front/up, jaw versus lower
neck, shoulders/wrists, visible prop axis and garment layers.319's crowd donor
torso faced opposite its head; continuous geometry still displayed the wrong
anatomical side. Inspect front and revealing reverse angles at actual shot scale.

Use immutable coordinate/normal snapshots when calculating surface changes.
319's sequential normal-based inflation fragmented the shirt; a no-inflation
control isolated the operation. A frozen-input calculation restored continuity,
but did not by itself establish believable clothing.

Prove one complete actor and its hardest pose/contact before duplicating it.
319 initially rebuilt all variants too early, increasing internal rework. Keeping
failed candidates away from the Director is necessary, but is not sufficient
evidence of efficient execution.

## Apply at motion and prop interfaces

Separate head-owned jaw/upper neck from torso-owned lower neck. A collar must not
hide a discontinuity. The current blended transform bridge is shot-authored and
must be revalidated for new anatomy, head motion or framing.

Test the visible shaft/handle, finger aperture, opposing thumb, palm, cuff and
shoulder together. A hidden inherited palm is a controller, not proof of a
convincing grip. Check unoccupied hands too.319's sampled spear-axis and full
timeline cuff checks supplement the rendered silhouette.

Sampling density must follow the fastest dependent action. Four-frame garment
samples created a109.5mm cuff error during the spear plant despite acceptable
sparse stills. Frame-dense sampling through that interval reduced the measured
maximum to under0.01mm across696 frames in16/19. This numerical result is neither
an anatomical nor an audiovisual pass.

## Apply when a local correction threatens a working design

Preserve working whole forms where possible.319's pointwise hair projection
introduced kinks; a replacement guide design changed the beard mass too much.
Both were rejected. The next candidate retained the earlier lock design and
changed only excess forward fall. Continuous strand guides must not jump
between disconnected ray hits. Fewer strands can be appropriate at supporting
shot scale, but counts and throughput cannot decide visual acceptance.

## Evidence and reuse boundary

Keep the actual historical recipe bytes, not only their hashes.319 builds01–06
lack complete source snapshots after later edits; exact recipe replay is not
claimed. Snapshots are retained from07 onward.

Check rights at file/dependency level. An unused local Viking-hair source had
conflicting CC0 descriptor and AGPL3 OBJ labels; it was not imported. Resolve
that source before reuse rather than carrying forward an earlier blanket claim.

Render-profile comparisons need identical frames and explicit changed-source
limits. On candidate16, two workers took331.4s versus296.1s serial for12 frames,
with9.59GB summed worker peaks. One worker was selected. This is not a universal
Mac or later-scene benchmark; a changed candidate receives its own serial check.

Direct motion/audio judgment remains with the Director under313. These findings
do not close pending real-time performance, ensemble or release decisions.
