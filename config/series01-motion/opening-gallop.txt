One continuous five-second cinematic shot in the same painted graphic-novel style. A bay horse gallops powerfully along the broad dirt road, dust trailing behind. Its legs cycle through push-off, brief suspension and landing with real forward travel. The woman rides low over its neck, knees bent, hips and torso absorbing the stride, hands following the reins. Starting high above and behind, the camera descends and advances along the horse's LEFT side, reaching a low lateral tracking view, then moving just ahead into a modest front-three-quarter reveal. Continuous spatial travel and ground parallax, never a stationary zoom or a cut. The horse stays on the road at racing speed. She keeps looking along the road, composed and determined, without turning her head toward camera. Indigo cloth and mane stream backward; warm light, bay coat and simple brown tack stay consistent. The same ink outlines, painted shading and ochre-blue palette persist throughout.
