# Demonstrator 01 — Stage A working plan

Status: development; not scope-frozen; no permission for Stages B–E is implied.

## What the audience should see

The Courier rides a dry mountain road toward a watchtower. A visible signal gives the ride purpose. The gallop must read clearly in a lateral tracking view before atmosphere or distant framing is used. Dust is the sole treatment. The provisional duration is 24 seconds at 24 fps (576 frames).

| Shot | Provisional duration | Story function and visible action |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 6 s | Wide establishing view: road leads toward a distant watchtower; horse/rider enter and advance screen-left to right. |
| 2 | 7 s | Lateral tracking at readable distance: authored gallop, moving ground, tack and rider coordination; thin hoof dust. |
| 3 | 5 s | Three-quarter rider/tower composition: a conspicuous tower signal, then rider head/upper-body orientation acknowledges it. |
| 4 | 6 s | Rear three-quarter resolve: rider continues along the same road toward the signal; hoof rhythm and edit give an ending. |

Signal implementation is provisional: a bright raised banner/light target whose change is plainly visible without a close facial shot. No dialogue, simulated cloth or lip sync. Stage B must establish the story in one complete inexpensive movie before surface polish.

## Reuse and gaps

- Reuse trusted credential-free Blender runner, source/hash bindings, packed-scene persistence, action inspection, FFmpeg full-clip playback and both existing ledgers.
- Prior asset import checks establish a workflow; their selected assets and rig-specific numerical gates do not qualify a new horse/rider.
- Reuse scene identity and revision concepts; do not refactor old campaigns or pretend their constrained schema already supports this film.
- New bounded work: read-only asset screening, matching forward travel to a source gait, saddle/rider contact integration, four shot controls and edit assembly. Production implementation waits for viable source motion.
- Existing Kenney character and castle geometry are not admitted as realistic film assets. No toy substitution.

## Stage A forecast contract

A 2–3 second (48–72 frame) integrated moving preview, plus a representative frame render, is the first deliverable. Forecast total film rendering from measured frame cost, resolution/sampling, and explicit rerender allowance. No trustworthy production time is available until the actual asset complexity and motion are inspected. Record lower/upper estimates separately from measurements. Reserve at least 5 GB disk headroom; about 11 GB was available at intake.

Proposed Stage A operating envelope: target 45–60 minutes of recorded active work including acquisition, safeguards, inspection, motion preview and packet; reassess at 20 minutes on any single diagnosis and 60 overall. Local renderer ceilings must be measured before production agreement. Await the Stage A gate before generating all four production shots.

Paid categories remain $10 feasibility, $40 implementation, $15 critique, $20 direction, $15 retry contingency; total $100 and 60 physical requests. All retries count; max three attempts per work item. No tools or images are enabled in the initial text-only API route. Source proposals are reviewed before becoming trusted repository code.
